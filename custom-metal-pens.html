MODEL SELECTION

Compare the Pen Before Comparing the Logo

A clear quotation starts with one identified model. Weight, mechanism, barrel shape, clip and finish can change the buyer experience even when two pens look similar in a small image.

Standard Stock Models

Start with readily available black, silver, navy or other confirmed stock colors. Special finishes, color matching or new structures require separate MOQ and sampling review.

Barrel and Clip Clearance

Measure the available marking area and keep the logo clear of clips, curves and moving parts. A simplified artwork version may be needed for small text or detailed symbols.

Mechanism and Refill

Confirm whether the selected model uses a twist, click or cap structure and identify the refill supplied with the sample. Do not assume all visually similar pens use the same mechanism or writing component.

Single Item or Gift Set

A pen can be supplied as a compact promotional item, packed in an individual case or combined with a notebook, PU folder, card holder or gift box after the final dimensions are checked.

CUSTOMIZATION

Logo, Sample, Packaging and Shipping Decisions

Laser Engraving

Engraving can create a precise, restrained mark without adding printed color. The visible result depends on the barrel material, coating and contrast revealed by the selected model.

Screen Printing

Printing may be suitable when the logo needs a visible single-color effect. Curved surfaces, small copy and coating adhesion should be checked on the sample.

MOQ and Sample

Many standard stock-model projects can be reviewed from around 100 pieces, but final MOQ depends on the exact pen, color, logo method and packaging. Approve the model, artwork size, position and visible effect before bulk production.

Packaging and Delivery

Choose an individual sleeve, standard pen box, PU-style case or fitted gift-set position according to the audience and budget. Provide the destination and target date so carton packing and delivery routes can be reviewed.

Buyer FAQ

Which logo method is suitable for a metal pen?

Laser engraving is often considered for a durable, restrained mark, while screen printing may suit a visible single-color logo. The suitable method depends on the barrel material, coating, artwork detail, logo color and selected model.

Can the complete company logo fit on the pen barrel?

It depends on the printable area and the detail in the artwork. A long company name, thin lines or a symbol plus text may need a simplified layout or a smaller approved version for the barrel.

What is the MOQ for custom metal pens?

Many standard stock-model projects can be planned from around 100 pieces, but final MOQ depends on pen model, stock color, logo method, packaging and current availability.

Can metal pens be packed individually?

Yes. Depending on presentation and budget, options may include an individual sleeve, standard pen box, PU-style pen case or placement inside a larger corporate gift box.

Can buyers approve a sample before bulk production?

Yes. Sample approval should confirm the exact pen model, color, mechanism, refill, logo size, logo position, visible logo effect and packaging.

What information is needed for a metal pen quote?

Provide quantity, preferred model or reference photo, stock color, logo artwork, logo method if known, packaging requirement, delivery country, postal code and target date.

Decisions to make before sampling

Keep homeowner and agent details separate

Use a consistent brokerage or property-project logo on the durable items. Put the individual agent message or property-specific information on a card when that makes small batches easier to manage. Confirm variable names and addresses before printing.

Choose items for the handover

A document folder is useful only if it fits the closing pack. A keyring should leave enough room for the actual keys and fob. For a mug-based welcome box, check handle clearance and protection rather than choosing packaging by its exterior appearance alone.

Use a sample to settle the layout

Review the components together: folder closure, pen orientation, key access, card position and box opening. Use a physical sample when feel, fit or print finish matters. The shown references demonstrate product directions, not a documented property closing or customer result.

Match packaging to storage and delivery

A presentation box suits an in-person key ceremony; a protective outer carton is a separate decision for courier delivery. If gifts go to several sales offices, confirm carton quantities and branch labels. If individual homes are the destinations, review packing and shipping scope first rather than assuming a bulk shipment includes residential fulfillment.

Buyer questions

What are useful closing gifts for real estate clients?

A document folder and pen can support the closing paperwork; a keyring suits the key handover; a mug, coaster and thank-you card create a home-welcome direction. Choose the items around the recipient and delivery method rather than combining every option.

Can a brokerage order closing gifts in bulk?

Yes, a brokerage can discuss a repeatable gift set for client handovers. Many standard set projects start planning around 100 sets, with the final minimum based on components, colors, logo versions and packaging. Share the first batch size and expected reorder pattern.

Can each closing gift have a different client name?

Variable names or messages can be reviewed for suitable items or cards. Confirm the personalization list, number of versions, approval method and packing labels. Individual artwork can change cost, minimum quantity and timing compared with one shared logo.

Are the document folders genuine leather?

The standard folder direction shown here uses PU material. PU should not be described as genuine leather. Confirm the selected material, texture, lining and construction in the quotation and sample before ordering.

Can gifts be shipped to several offices or client addresses?

Share the number of destinations and required delivery dates before quotation. Multi-address packing, labels and shipping scope need a separate review. A bulk order to one office does not automatically include delivery to individual client homes.

Are open-house giveaways different from closing gift boxes?

Open-house giveaways are usually distributed to a broader visitor group, so compact pens, bags or keyrings can be considered. A closing gift is prepared for a specific client milestone and may include coordinated packaging, a document folder and a personal card.

Prepare your gift quote

Share whether the gifts are for a brokerage closing, developer key handover, open house or client thank-you. Include quantity per batch, target budget, document or key dimensions, logo file, gift items, packaging preference, destination and required arrival date. For individual personalization, provide the number of artwork versions before quotation.

Sample cost, production timing and freight are confirmed against the selected specification. Review sample approval and the order timeline before setting the final schedule.

Choose the method around the item, not just the logo

A logo that looks excellent on a notebook may not behave the same way on stainless steel, fabric, silicone, paper, ceramic or coated plastic. Corporate gift branding should begin with the product material and the user experience. Some items need a subtle mark, while trade show giveaways may need higher visibility. A premium client gift may benefit from a restrained engraving or debossed packaging rather than a large printed logo.

Sendora Gift can discuss common options such as screen printing, UV printing, laser engraving, embroidery, embossing, debossing, labels, stickers, sleeves, insert cards and branded gift boxes. The available method depends on the selected product and supplier capability for that item. The final recommendation should be made after reviewing artwork, quantity and sample requirements.

How different branding methods are usually used

Printing is often considered for flat or slightly curved surfaces where color visibility matters. Laser engraving is often useful on metal, wood or certain coated items when a durable, understated mark is preferred. Embroidery can work for fabric products such as bags, towels or apparel when the design is suitable for thread. Embossing or debossing can support a premium feel on suitable paper, leather-like or box materials.

Packaging branding is a separate decision. A small product logo can be paired with stronger identity on the box, card, sleeve or paper bag. This is useful when the buyer wants the item to remain tasteful but still wants the unboxing experience to feel branded. For distributor projects, packaging may also help identify campaign versions or event batches.

Approval details that prevent branding mistakes

Buyers should provide vector artwork when possible, plus brand color references and any clear-space rules. Logo size, position and color should be checked on a mockup before sample production. If the logo includes thin lines or small text, the decoration method may need adjustment. A sample or detailed production photo can help confirm whether the mark looks acceptable on the actual item.

Logo methods affect MOQ, sample cost, production process and sometimes packaging. They should not be treated as a last-minute add-on. The earlier the method is discussed, the easier it is to choose products that support the brand style and approval process.

Decision notes for logo approval

Logo approval should consider how the recipient will use the product. A large event logo may work on a tote bag but feel too loud on an executive notebook. A subtle engraving may feel premium but may not be visible enough for a trade show giveaway. The right decision depends on audience, channel and brand tone.

Buyers should also confirm whether the product or the packaging should carry the main brand message. A smaller item logo combined with a stronger box, sleeve or insert card can preserve usefulness while still making the campaign feel custom. This choice should be reviewed before sample approval.

FAQ

Answers for Buyers

Which logo method is best for corporate gifts?

There is no single best method. The choice depends on product material, logo artwork, quantity, brand style, packaging plan and durability expectations.

Can Sendora Gift put branding on packaging instead of the product?

Yes. Branding can often be placed on boxes, sleeves, stickers, insert cards or paper bags when that better fits the gift style. Final options depend on the packaging direction.

Do I need a vector logo file?

Vector artwork is preferred because it makes logo review and production setup clearer. If only another format is available, Sendora Gift can review it and explain what may be needed.

Does Sendora Gift publish fixed MOQ or price tables?

No. MOQ and price depend on the selected products, quantity, logo method, packaging and destination. Sendora Gift confirms those details after reviewing the buyer requirements.

Can buyers request samples before bulk production?

Yes. Sample options can be discussed after the product combination, artwork and packaging direction are clear. Sample timing and cost are confirmed project by project.

Can the product combination be changed?

Yes. Buyers can start from a reference gift set and adjust items, logo placement, packaging or delivery requirements based on the audience and budget.

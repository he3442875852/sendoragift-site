Custom Luggage Tags with Logo for Business Travel Gifts

Custom luggage tags are compact travel accessories for business travel programs, incentive trips, event welcome packs and distributor promotional campaigns. Procurement Notes for Custom Luggage Tags

Luggage tags should be selected by material, strap type, privacy cover, card window, logo position and packaging. A privacy cover can make the item feel more business-appropriate when personal information is visible.

For event or travel programs, buyers should confirm whether the tag ships alone, inside a travel kit, with a passport holder or with custom packaging.

Quote Inputs Buyers Should Prepare

  • PU style and privacy cover requirement
  • Logo method: embossing, printing or foil stamping
  • Quantity and color preference
  • Individual packaging or travel kit packing
  • Destination country, postal code and target delivery date

Use Cases, Materials and Styles

PU Luggage Tags

PU luggage tags are common for corporate travel gifts because they are compact, easy to pack and suitable for subtle logo decoration. Buyers can compare standard shapes, strap styles and color options before requesting a sample.

Privacy Cover

A privacy cover helps hide the traveler information card while keeping it accessible. It is useful for business travel, conferences, incentive trips and employee mobility programs.

Business Travel and Event Use

Luggage tags can be used for executive travel gifts, conference welcome bags, international meeting kits, trade show giveaways and company travel programs.

Color Direction

Black, brown, navy, grey and other business colors are common. Bright colors can work for event visibility, but availability and MOQ should be confirmed with the chosen tag style.

Logo, MOQ, Sampling, Packaging and Shipping

Logo Customization

Logo methods may include embossing, debossing, foil stamping or printing depending on the PU surface and artwork. Buyers should confirm whether the logo appears on the front, back or privacy flap.

MOQ Factors

Many standard stock-color projects may start from approximately 100 pieces, but final MOQ depends on the selected luggage tag, color, logo method, strap style and packaging.

Sample Process

A sample should confirm material feel, stitching, strap attachment, privacy cover movement, logo sharpness and whether the information card fits correctly.

Packaging and Shipping

Tags can be bulk packed, individually bagged, inserted into travel kits or placed in small gift boxes. Shipping quotes need carton details, destination country, postal code and deadline.

Buyer FAQ

Can luggage tags include a privacy cover?

Yes. Privacy cover options can be reviewed based on the selected PU luggage tag style and buyer requirements.

Which logo methods suit PU luggage tags?

Embossing, debossing, printing and foil stamping may be available depending on the PU surface, logo detail and quantity.

Are luggage tags suitable for event gifts?

Yes. They work for travel-themed events, conference welcome packs, incentive trips and business travel programs.

Can buyers approve a sample before bulk production?

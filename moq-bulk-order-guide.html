MOQ & Bulk Order Guide

Plan custom corporate gift orders with clearer expectations for MOQ, pricing, samples, logo setup and packaging.

What Affects MOQ?

Minimum order quantity depends on the product, material, customization method, packaging type and supplier production requirements. A simple logo item may have a different MOQ from a fully custom gift box.

Product Type

Stock items with logo customization usually have more flexible quantities than fully custom-molded or custom-colored products.

Logo Method

Printing, embroidery, engraving, UV printing and packaging processes may each have setup costs or minimum batch requirements.

Packaging

Custom rigid boxes, inserts, sleeves, paper bags and cards can affect MOQ and lead time.

Information Needed for Bulk Pricing

  • Product name or reference image
  • Quantity range
  • Logo artwork and colors
  • Packaging requirements
  • Target budget per item or per set
  • Delivery country and target date

Sample Orders

Samples help confirm product quality, logo position and packaging before bulk production. Sample timing and cost vary by product and customization.

Why MOQ Changes by Product

Minimum order quantity is affected by material, mold, logo method, packaging, color customization and supplier production setup. Simple stock items with logo printing usually have lower MOQ than fully custom products or custom packaging.

Lower MOQ Items

Pens, notebooks, mugs, lanyards, tote bags and stock bottles often support more flexible quantities for sampling or smaller campaigns.

Higher MOQ Items

Custom colors, molded products, apparel, fully custom boxes and special materials may require higher quantities to control unit cost.

Bulk Order Savings

Larger orders can reduce unit cost because setup, printing and packing work are spread across more pieces.

How to Ask for an Accurate MOQ

  • Share product type, estimated quantity, logo method and packaging needs.
  • Tell us whether you can use stock colors or need custom brand colors.
  • Confirm target delivery date so production and shipping options can be checked together.

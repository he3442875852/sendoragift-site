Corporate Gift MOQ and Bulk Order Guide

Understand why minimum order quantity changes across standard products, logo methods, custom colors, packaging and mixed gift sets before requesting a bulk quote.

Why MOQ Differs by Product

MOQ is not one universal number for every corporate gift. It changes according to the selected product, material, logo method, order quantity, artwork, packaging and destination requirements.

Ready-Made Products

Standard ready-made items with logo customization may allow more flexible quantities because the base product already exists.

Custom-Made Structures

Custom colors, special molds, new gift box structures or custom inserts may require higher quantity review because setup and materials are project-specific.

Logo Methods

Printing, engraving, embroidery, UV printing, embossing and packaging branding can each have different setup requirements.

Sample Order Versus Bulk Order

Sample Review

Samples or sample photos help confirm product model, material, color, logo effect and packaging before bulk approval.

Mixed Gift Sets

Gift sets may combine products with different MOQs, so quantity planning should consider every item plus the box, insert and carton packing.

Repeat Orders

Repeat projects are easier when product references, logo files, packaging details and carton requirements are kept consistent.

Information Required to Confirm MOQ

  • Product type or reference link
  • Estimated quantity and target budget
  • Logo file, logo size and preferred method
  • Stock color or custom color requirement
  • Gift box, sleeve, card, bag or carton needs
  • Delivery country, destination and requested timeline

FAQ

Does every corporate gift product have the same MOQ?

No. MOQ depends on the selected product, material, logo method, artwork, packaging structure and order quantity.

Can we request samples before a bulk order?

Sample options depend on the product and customization method. Samples or sample photos can help confirm product quality, logo effect and packaging before bulk production.

Do custom colors or packaging change MOQ?

They can. Custom colors, custom boxes, inserts, sleeves and special packaging usually require separate MOQ review from the product itself.

Can mixed-product gift sets use different MOQs?

Yes. Each item in a mixed gift set may have its own MOQ, so quantity planning should consider the highest-risk product or packaging component.

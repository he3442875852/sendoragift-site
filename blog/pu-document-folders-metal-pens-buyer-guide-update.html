New Buyer Guides for PU Document Folders and Metal Pens

More specific guidance for product fit, logo approval and presentation packing before a custom business-gift order.

Read the Folder GuideRead the Pen Guide

By Sendora Gift · Published October 8, 2026

Sendora Gift has expanded its buyer resources for custom PU document folders and custom metal pens. The updated pages explain how to define a usable product specification, compare logo methods and review the finished presentation before a bulk order is approved.

The guidance is intended for procurement teams, HR buyers, event organizers and promotional-product distributors comparing practical business gifts. It adds detail to the buying brief so a reference photograph can lead to a clearer quotation and sample review.

What Is New?

The folder guide now separates opening folders, file pouches and presentation portfolios, with checks for paper fit, capacity and logo alignment. The pen guide compares engraving and printing, explains two-logo placement and adds checks for visibility in the final case. Both pages include RFQ fields buyers can copy.

PU Folders: Define the Structure and the Loaded Fit

A photograph alone may not show whether a folder holds loose papers, a notebook or a complete meeting pack. Start by choosing the structure: an opening folder, a file pouch or a portfolio with internal pockets. Then confirm the closure, document capacity and any pen loop or card holder needed for the intended use.

A4 and A5 identify paper formats; they do not specify the outside dimensions of a finished folder. Ask for the actual internal and external measurements of the selected model. Check that papers fit without bending and that the closure still works when the intended contents are loaded.

The updated guide also explains logo alignment. A centered mark should be measured against an agreed reference edge, flap or seam. For debossing, inspect the depth and readability on the actual PU surface instead of relying only on a flat digital proof.

Metal Pens: Approve the Method and Each Logo Position

A similar-looking pen may have a different coating, mechanism, refill or marking area. Confirm the exact model and finish before comparing decoration methods. Laser engraving produces contrast according to the material and coating; it does not automatically reproduce a specified printed color.

If a project needs two logos, provide separate artwork and identify the front and tail positions. Record the intended size and viewing direction, including how the pen should look when its clip faces upward. Each position needs its own review for clearance from joints, clips and moving parts.

Finally, approve the pen in its intended sleeve or presentation case. A retaining band or recess may cover a correct mark. An open-case photograph helps show whether the logo remains visible and whether the pen stays secure without pressure on its mechanism.

Turn the Buying Points Into an Approval Record

Keep the product specification, artwork revision and packaging reference together. The new comparison points can be used as a short review record rather than separate comments scattered across messages.

Prepare a Quote Brief for the Selected Products

Share the intended use and arrival date alongside the product details. A conference handout, employee kit and client presentation may need different packing, logo coverage and distribution plans even when they use the same basic items.

RFQ fields to copy: Product model or reference photo: ___; folder structure and dimensions: ___; pen model, finish and refill: ___; quantity: ___; logo files, sizes and positions: ___; decoration method: ___; case or gift-box requirements: ___; destination and postal code: ___; required arrival date: ___; sample requirement: ___.

Final availability, MOQ, sample cost, production timing and shipping are confirmed for the actual combination. The resource update does not establish a universal minimum quantity, fixed price or guaranteed lead time. Send the completed brief through the project inquiry form to discuss suitable options.

Buyer Questions About the Update

What changed in the PU folder and metal pen buyer guides?

The guides now compare product structures and logo methods in more detail, explain folder fit and pen-case checks, and provide RFQ fields buyers can copy into a project brief.

Do A4 and A5 specify the finished folder dimensions?

No. A4 and A5 describe paper formats. Confirm the actual internal and external folder dimensions, document capacity and closure clearance for the selected model.

Can laser engraving reproduce a specified logo color on a metal pen?

Engraving contrast depends on the pen material, coating and marking process. A specified printed color needs a suitable printing method and a separate sample approval.

Trade show swag has a simple test: will the visitor still use it after the event? The best items either solve an event-day problem or become useful after the attendee returns to the office.

Swag People Are More Likely to Keep

  • Tote bags that help carry materials during the event
  • Notebooks and pens for meetings and sessions
  • Lanyards and badge holders when the event needs them
  • Tumblers or bottles for longer post-event use
  • Small chargers or tech accessories for higher-value visitors
  • QR insert cards that connect the gift to a campaign or offer

Match the Swag to the Event Context

A sunny outdoor event may need fans, bottles or sunscreen-related accessories. A rainy city event may favor umbrellas or waterproof pouches. A tech conference may value chargers, laptop stickers or cable organizers. Context-specific gifts feel more thoughtful and less generic.

Use QR Cards for Lead Follow-Up

A small insert card can connect the physical giveaway to your digital campaign. Add a QR code, event landing page, product guide or meeting booking link. This makes the gift easier to measure and gives sales teams a natural follow-up angle.

Trade Show Swag FAQ

What trade show swag do people keep?

People often keep practical event items such as tote bags, notebooks, pens, drinkware, chargers, lanyards, badge holders and useful location-specific giveaways.

What is good swag for a B2B trade show booth?

Good B2B booth swag is lightweight, useful during the event, easy for staff to distribute and connected to a follow-up action such as a QR card.

How should trade show swag be packed?

Mass giveaways can be bulk packed for booth teams, while VIP visitor kits can use paper bags, insert cards or gift boxes.

Custom Logo Printing Methods for Corporate Gifts

Understand which branding method fits your product, budget and visual effect.

Published June 12, 2026 | Logo Customization

Logo customization is one of the most important parts of a corporate gift project. The best method depends on the product material, logo design, color requirements, quantity and budget.

Screen Printing

Screen printing is widely used for bags, apparel, plastic products, notebooks and simple promotional items. It is cost-effective for larger quantities and works best for simple logos with solid colors.

  • Good for tote bags, T-shirts, notebooks and plastic items
  • Suitable for simple one-color or few-color logos
  • Cost-effective for bulk corporate gifts

UV Printing

UV printing can support more colorful designs and is often used on flat surfaces. It is useful when the logo includes gradients, multiple colors or detailed artwork.

  • Good for notebooks, boxes, acrylic items and some drinkware surfaces
  • Supports full-color artwork
  • Works best on suitable flat or slightly curved areas

Laser Engraving

Laser engraving creates a clean, durable and premium effect. It is often used on metal pens, stainless steel bottles, tumblers, keychains and executive accessories.

  • Good for stainless steel, metal, bamboo and some leather-style materials
  • Durable and professional appearance
  • Best for executive gifts and premium drinkware

Embroidery

Embroidery is suitable for textile products such as caps, bags, towels, polo shirts and some apparel items. It creates a textured effect that feels more durable than printing.

  • Good for caps, tote bags, backpacks and apparel
  • Best for simple logos with clear shapes
  • Not ideal for very small text or complex gradients

Heat Transfer

Heat transfer is often used for apparel, bags and fabric items when more colors or detailed graphics are needed. It can be a good choice for event merchandise and team apparel.

Embossing and Debossing

Embossing and debossing are used on paper packaging, leather-style notebooks, gift boxes and premium accessories. These methods create a subtle, high-end brand effect.

Buyer tip: Send vector artwork when possible. AI, EPS, PDF or high-resolution logo files help suppliers confirm logo size, color and placement faster.

How to Choose the Right Method

For budget giveaways, screen printing is often practical. For premium drinkware, laser engraving can look more professional. For fabric bags and caps, embroidery may be better. For full-color logos, UV printing or heat transfer may be more suitable.

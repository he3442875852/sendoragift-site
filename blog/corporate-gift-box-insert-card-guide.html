Corporate Gift Box Insert Card Guide

Plan insert cards for welcome kits, client thank-you boxes and event gifts without turning the article into a general gift-box sourcing page.

A corporate gift box insert card is the message layer of the gift. It can welcome a new employee, thank a client, explain an event campaign or direct recipients to a QR code without forcing every product to carry a large logo.

This article focuses on insert cards only. For broader box structures and sleeves, review custom gift box packaging.

Common Insert Card Purposes

  • Welcome card: used in onboarding kits or new-hire gifts.
  • Appreciation card: used for client thank-you gifts or renewal moments.
  • Instruction card: used when the set includes several items or a QR code.
  • Campaign card: used for events, product launches or partner programs.

Card Size and Box Placement

Insert card size should match the box interior, product height and packing method. A card placed on top creates the first message. A smaller card beside the products may feel less formal but easier to pack. Confirm the layout before approving production.

Printed Message Versus Personalization

A shared printed message is simpler for bulk orders. Personalized recipient names or variable details may require additional artwork handling and packing controls. For employee programs, see employee welcome kits. For client messages, compare client thank-you gift boxes.

Logo, Brand Color and QR Code Use

Insert cards can carry a logo, short message, QR code, campaign URL or contact information. Keep the layout readable and confirm whether the QR code must be tested before printing. Brand colors should be supplied as artwork references rather than described loosely.

Artwork and Packing Confirmation

Card text, spelling, logo position, QR code, color references and box placement should be approved before printing. Paper and finish choices can be discussed without making unsupported material or certification claims.

Insert card RFQ checklist: card purpose, message text, logo file, card size, box placement, QR code, quantity, gift box type and approval contact.

Corporate Gift Box Insert Card Guide FAQ

What is a corporate gift box insert card used for?

It can carry a welcome message, thank-you note, campaign explanation, instruction, QR code or contact details inside the gift box.

Can insert cards be personalized by recipient?

Personalization may be possible depending on artwork handling and packing requirements, but it should be confirmed before production planning.

Where should the insert card be placed in the box?

Placement depends on box size, product height and the desired first impression. Many projects place the card on top or beside the products.

What should be approved before insert card printing?

Approve the message, spelling, logo position, QR code, brand colors, card size and box placement before printing.

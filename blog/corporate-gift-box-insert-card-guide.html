Gift Box Insert Card Guide for Corporate Gifts

Plan gift box insert cards for corporate gifts, including welcome cards, thank-you cards, QR cards, event instruction cards and brand message cards.

Request a Quote Browse Products

A gift box insert card is a small detail that can change how a corporate gift is understood. It explains the purpose of the gift, connects the products to the brand, and can guide recipients to an event page, onboarding resource or thank-you message.

This guide is for HR teams, client success teams, event organizers, brand teams and distributors planning branded cards inside custom gift boxes.

Who This Guide Is For

Use this guide when you need a practical B2B gift plan that can move from idea to quotation, sample approval, logo confirmation and bulk order planning without unsupported claims or unclear expectations.

Recommended Product Combinations

  • Welcome card for employee onboarding kits
  • Thank-you card for client appreciation boxes
  • QR card for event instructions, surveys or landing pages
  • Brand message card for premium executive gift sets

1. Choose the Card Purpose

Start by deciding what the card needs to do. A welcome card should make new employees feel included. A thank-you card should sound sincere and concise. A QR card should tell recipients exactly why they should scan. An event instruction card should support registration, agenda access or booth activity.

2. Keep the Message Short

Most insert cards work best with a headline, two to four short sentences and a clear signature or brand mark. Long paragraphs are easy to ignore. If the project needs detailed instructions, use a QR code and keep the printed card focused on the main action.

3. Design and Material Notes

Card size should fit the box layout without covering key products. Common options include matte cards, coated cards, folded cards and small envelope cards. For premium boxes, keep the design clean and use the card to add warmth rather than heavy advertising.

4. QR Code Planning

If the card includes a QR code, test it before printing. Confirm the landing page is live, mobile-friendly and appropriate for the recipient. Add short text near the QR code so recipients know whether it opens a welcome page, event guide, survey or product story.

5. Approval Checklist

Confirm spelling, logo file, card size, paper direction, QR code, brand colors, recipient wording and box placement before production. A card error is small in size but highly visible when every recipient opens the gift.

Quote checklist: quantity, budget per set, delivery country, target delivery date, logo file, product direction and packaging preference.

Gift Box Insert Card Guide for Corporate Gifts FAQ

What should a gift box insert card say?

It should explain the gift purpose with a short welcome, thank-you, event instruction or brand message.

Can insert cards include QR codes?

Yes. QR cards are useful for onboarding pages, event guides, surveys or campaign landing pages.

What size should an insert card be?

Card size should match the box layout and product position. Confirm size during packaging layout approval.

Should the card be branded?

Yes, but the message should remain clear and recipient-focused, not only promotional.

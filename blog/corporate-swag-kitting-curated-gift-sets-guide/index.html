Corporate Swag Kitting: How to Plan Custom Corporate Gift Sets

By Sendora Gift · Published October 24, 2026

Corporate swag kitting means bringing several branded products together in one coordinated package. A kit might contain a notebook, pen, mug and message card, with the product selection, logo placement, packaging and delivery arranged as one project.

For a B2B buyer, the main decisions are what recipients will use, how many sets are needed, how the items fit together and where the finished gifts must arrive. This guide explains how to prepare those details before requesting a quote.

What does a corporate swag kitting project include?

A kitting project can cover product selection, logo customization, packaging, assembly and shipment preparation. The quotation should identify which of these services are included and which are charged separately.

For example, a notebook-and-pen set requires more decisions than choosing two product photos. The buyer needs to confirm the notebook size and stock color, pen model, logo method on each item, presentation box, item positioning and protection inside the shipping carton.

Before ordering, agree on one written specification for the complete set. This gives the product, packaging and packing teams the same reference.

What affects the minimum order quantity?

Around 100 sets is a useful starting point for planning many standard custom gift-set projects. It is not a fixed minimum for every combination. The final MOQ depends on the individual products, stock colors, logo method and packaging structure.

An existing pen model with a laser-engraved logo may have different requirements from a specially colored product or a newly developed box. Each component needs to be checked before the complete set can be quoted.

For smaller quantities, ask about available product models and stock colors. Keeping one core combination and one packaging format can make the project easier to arrange. Share your actual quantity even if it is below the planning reference so suitable options can be reviewed.

How to compare quotations for corporate gift sets

A fair comparison uses the same product and delivery specification. A lower unit price may leave out logo setup, packaging, assembly, protective cartons or freight.

Ask each supplier to confirm these cost items:

  1. Products: exact model, material, size, color and quantity for each item.
  2. Branding: logo method, number of positions, artwork requirements and any setup charges.
  3. Packaging: box construction, dimensions, insert or divider, printed card and outer cartons.
  4. Samples: what is included, sample charges and sample shipping.
  5. Assembly: whether arranging the products, adding cards and packing the sets is included.
  6. Delivery: destination, shipping method, quoted trade terms and any duties or taxes included or excluded.

State whether your target budget is for the products alone or for the complete delivered project. This helps avoid comparing a product-only quote with a delivered gift-set quote.

Example planning brief: 100 client thank-you sets

Suppose you need 100 sets containing a ceramic mug, PU coaster and thank-you card for delivery to one office. A useful brief identifies the mug size and finish, coaster shape, logo files, card wording, packaging preference, destination postal code and required arrival date.

Then compare two packing options: a presentation box with fitted support, and a simpler box with protective dividers. Review how each option protects the mug, holds the coaster and affects carton size. This is an illustrative planning example, not a completed customer project or a fixed-price offer.

Select packaging after confirming the products

Product dimensions should guide the box layout. Check the mug handle, notebook thickness, pen length and any straps or attachments before approving the final structure.

A decorative gift box is not automatically suitable as a shipping carton. Fragile products may need dividers, cushioning and protective outer packing. Paper filler can improve presentation, but it should not be assumed to secure a ceramic mug by itself.

To control shipping costs, compare the packed dimensions and weight of the complete set. Avoid unnecessary empty space while leaving enough room for protective materials. See our custom gift box packaging options.

Match the logo method to each item

Different surfaces need different branding methods. One logo file does not mean every item should use the same application process.

  • PU notebooks, folders and travel accessories: review debossing, printing or foil stamping for the selected surface.
  • Metal pens and accessories: consider engraving or printing, with the position checked against clips, curves and working parts.
  • Drinkware: confirm the available decoration area, finish and logo method for the chosen model.
  • Textile items: review embroidery or printing against fabric texture, seams and logo detail.

Provide vector artwork when available. Approve logo size, orientation, position and color separately for each item and for the box. A digital mockup helps review placement; a physical sample helps assess the actual material and finish.

Review a complete sample before bulk production

An individual product sample does not show how the finished set will fit together. Where a complete sample is arranged, review the contents, branding and packaging as one unit.

  • Check product models, sizes, colors and quantities against the specification.
  • Confirm logo direction, readable details and placement on every item.
  • Review card wording, spelling and the box logo position.
  • Close the box and check that products fit without pressure or loose movement.
  • Review the agreed inspection points and shipping-carton arrangement.

Sample availability, charges and timing are confirmed after the project is reviewed. Record approvals and any changes in writing. Our sample approval guide explains what to check.

Plan production and international delivery together

Share your required arrival date at the start of the project. The schedule needs to allow for product selection, artwork review, sample preparation and approval, bulk production, packing and transport.

Production timing depends on the selected products, quantity, customization and packaging. Confirm the production start conditions and dispatch estimate in the quotation rather than counting from the first inquiry.

For shipping, provide the country, city, postal code and whether the destination is an office, event venue or freight forwarder. Express, air freight or sea freight options can be reviewed according to the order and destination. Sea freight requires a route-specific estimate; it should not be treated as a fixed worldwide transit time.

If a delivered-duty-paid arrangement is proposed, confirm availability and the included charges for that specific route. If gifts need to go to multiple addresses, ask for destination coverage, packing requirements, recipient-data handling and a separate fulfillment quotation before committing to that plan.

Questions buyers ask about custom corporate gift sets

What is corporate swag kitting?

Corporate swag kitting is the process of combining several branded products into one coordinated package. A project can include product selection, logo customization, packaging, assembly and shipment preparation, with the included services stated in the quotation.

What is the minimum order quantity for custom corporate gift sets?

Around 100 sets is a useful planning reference for many standard projects. Final MOQ depends on the selected products, stock colors, logo methods and packaging. Share your actual quantity so suitable options can be reviewed.

Can we change the items in a displayed gift set?

Yes. A displayed combination can be used as a starting point. The revised products, packaging fit, MOQ, price and timing need to be reviewed for your quantity and destination.

Can our logo be added to both the products and the box?

Yes, subject to the selected materials and available decoration methods. Confirm the artwork, logo size, orientation, position and color separately for each product and the packaging.

Can we review a sample before bulk production?

Sample options can be arranged after product selection and artwork review. Sample scope, cost and timing are confirmed for the project. Review the product finish, logo effect and packaging fit before approving bulk production.

How long does a custom gift-set order take?

The schedule includes specification review, artwork, samples, approval, bulk production, packing and shipping. Timing is confirmed for the selected combination, quantity and destination. Share your required arrival date before placing the order.

What information is needed for an accurate quotation?

Send your estimated quantity, recipient type, preferred products, target budget, logo file, packaging needs, destination country and postal code, required arrival date and sample requirement. Clarify whether the budget includes shipping and taxes.

Can gift sets be shipped internationally?

International shipping can be planned after reviewing the products, packed dimensions, weight, destination and deadline. The quotation should state the route, trade terms and which freight, duty and tax charges are included.

Can gifts be delivered to individual employee addresses?

Ask for a project-specific review before planning individual deliveries. Destination coverage, address handling, packing protection, shipment charges and any fulfillment fees need to be confirmed separately.

How can we control the total project cost?

Start with a compact combination of useful items, compare stock colors, keep logo applications clear and choose packaging that fits the products. Compare quotes using the same specification and include samples, assembly, protective packing and delivery in the budget review.

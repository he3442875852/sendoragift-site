Golf Tournament Gift Sets: A Sponsor Logo Checklist

A tournament pack may involve several sponsors, recipient groups and product surfaces. Separate the artwork allocation from the item count, then approve the complete kit against one clear logo and packing record.

By Sendora Gift · Updated 31 October 2026 · About the company

Start with the answerRequest options

How should sponsor logos be organized on golf tournament gifts?

Create a matrix listing each product or pack surface, its assigned logo, artwork version, proposed method and approver. Record quantities for player, volunteer, sponsor and VIP variants. Approve each logo surface and one complete packed reference for each kit version before releasing the final allocation.

Build a surface-by-surface sponsor matrix

Begin with the items actually being quoted. A small accessory, towel and outer pack offer different branding areas. Assign the event mark and sponsor marks intentionally; do not assume each item can carry the entire sponsor board.

Name the approver for each mark and layout

A sponsor may approve its own logo while the organizer approves the combined layout. Keep those decisions distinct. A correct sponsor file can still be placed in the wrong order or assigned to the wrong recipient group.

  • Collect current artwork from each brand owner.
  • Confirm sponsor hierarchy, minimum logo size or exclusion rules supplied by the organizer.
  • Record who approves the complete layout and who can request changes.
  • Identify the final file version for each product and pack surface.
  • Ask for a schedule review if artwork changes after approval.

Use the artwork handoff brief to keep file, placement and version together. An old event photograph does not establish permission to use its sponsor marks.

Separate kit counts from component counts

Count complete kits first, then calculate components and variants. Player, volunteer and sponsor packs may have different contents. Logo impressions, individual accessories and recipient totals are not interchangeable counts.

Use sample evidence for the questions it can answer

A physical photo or video helps inspect visible appearance and arrangement. It cannot establish material grade, transit performance, attendance or sponsor results. Name such requirements in the specification and agree the evidence needed.

Approve the packed kit and delivery handoff

  1. Confirm final contents and recipient-version quantities.
  2. Compare each logo surface with the approved matrix.
  3. Check a complete loaded reference for each kit version.
  4. Agree component counts, card versions, labels and carton marks.
  5. Provide the venue or warehouse destination, receiving window and contact requirements.
  6. Check the quoted production and shipping plan against required arrival.

The event date is not the delivery date. Include the organizer’s receiving and distribution requirements, and confirm what the proposed schedule covers. No universal production or freight time is implied. See delivery planning.

Golf sponsor gift questions

Can all sponsor logos go on every item?

Only if the surfaces, artwork and decoration support it. A clear allocation across products, card and outer pack may be more workable; approve the actual layout.

How should player and VIP packs be quoted?

List each kit version, contents and quantity separately. Identify shared components and different logos, cards or presentation.

Can we reuse an earlier event’s artwork?

Obtain current files and permission from the brand owner. An old image or file does not establish approval for a new event or sponsor hierarchy.

Does the sample video prove a delivered event order?

It is a physical sample reference for visible presentation, rather than evidence of a completed tournament order or sponsor outcome.

Custom Gift Box Packaging Guide for Corporate Gifts

Use packaging to make branded gift sets feel more complete, premium and memorable.

Published June 12, 2026 | Gift Packaging

Packaging is often the difference between a simple product bundle and a professional corporate gift set. For client gifts, employee kits and event boxes, packaging helps organize items, protect products and communicate your brand.

Common Packaging Options

  • Rigid gift boxes: suitable for premium client gifts, executive boxes and holiday sets.
  • Paper boxes: practical for medium-budget gift sets and employee kits.
  • Mailer boxes: useful for remote employee programs and direct shipping.
  • Paper bags: simple option for events, conferences and hand-delivered gifts.
  • Sleeves and labels: cost-effective way to add brand identity.

Insert Cards and Greeting Cards

A short message card can make the gift feel more personal. For employee onboarding, the card can welcome new hires. For client appreciation, it can include a thank-you message or campaign note.

Interior Layout and Product Protection

If a gift set includes multiple products, interior structure matters. Paper inserts, foam inserts, dividers or product sleeves help keep items in place during shipping and improve the unboxing experience.

Branding the Packaging

Packaging can be customized with logo printing, hot stamping, labels, sleeves, color matching and branded cards. The right approach depends on quantity, budget and desired visual effect.

Buyer tip: If you need custom packaging, confirm product dimensions before finalizing the box size. This avoids loose packaging or damaged items during transport.

When to Choose Premium Packaging

Premium packaging is worth considering for VIP clients, executive gifts, holiday appreciation gifts and campaigns where brand impression matters. For mass giveaways, simpler packaging may be more cost-effective.

What to Share With Your Supplier

  • Product list and size of each item
  • Quantity and target budget
  • Logo file and brand colors
  • Delivery method and destination
  • Whether gifts are handed out or shipped individually

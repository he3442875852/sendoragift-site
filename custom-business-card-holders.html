Procurement Notes for Business Card Holders

Business card holders work well when a buyer wants a small branded item that feels business-relevant and easy to distribute. They can be purchased alone or paired with notebooks, pens, desk mats and packaging.

The main buying decisions are holder style, capacity, PU texture, color, logo method, packaging and whether the item will be used as an office gift, event giveaway or part of a larger gift set.

Updated 2026-08-04

Quote Inputs Buyers Should Prepare

  • PU card holder style and capacity
  • Logo method: embossing, debossing or printing
  • Stock color or business color direction
  • Individual packaging or gift set combination
  • Quantity, delivery country and target date

Use Cases, Materials and Styles

Standard PU Card Holders

Standard PU business card holders are practical for office gifting because they are compact, easy to brand and suitable for daily business use. Buyers should confirm whether the holder is for desk storage, pocket use or event distribution.

Corporate Office Gifts

A business card holder can be used for sales teams, new employees, account managers, conference speakers, distributor gifts and client office kits.

Compact Promotional Item

For trade shows or meetings, a card holder can be a compact promotional item when a simple branded accessory is more appropriate than a large gift box.

Color and Packaging

Black, brown, navy, grey and other business colors are common. Packaging can be simple individual bags, small boxes or part of a coordinated desk accessory set.

Logo, MOQ, Sampling, Packaging and Shipping

Logo Customization

Logo embossing, debossing or printing can be reviewed according to PU texture and logo detail. A subtle front logo often works well for office gifts.

MOQ Factors

Many standard stock-color projects may start from approximately 100 pieces, but final MOQ depends on holder style, color, logo method, packaging and whether other items are included.

Sample Process

A sample should confirm card capacity, opening direction, stitching, logo position, logo clarity and packaging fit before bulk production.

Bulk Purchase Notes

For distributor or event orders, buyers should confirm carton quantity, barcode or label needs, destination, postal code, delivery window and whether products ship to one or multiple addresses.

Buyer FAQ

What material is common for business card holders?

Standard PU card holders are common for corporate office gifts and compact promotional items.

Can business card holders be part of a gift set?

Yes. They can be paired with notebooks, pens, desk mats, travel accessories or custom packaging when the gift set size and budget are clear.

Which logo methods are suitable?

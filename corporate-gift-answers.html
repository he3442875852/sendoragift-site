DIRECT ANSWERS

Common Corporate Gift Questions

What does Sendora Gift do?

Sendora Gift helps B2B buyers plan custom corporate gift sets, employee welcome kits, client gifts, event giveaways, logo branding, packaging, sample approval and international delivery coordination.

Who is Sendora Gift best for?

Sendora Gift is best for procurement teams, HR teams, event organizers, sourcing agents and promotional product distributors planning custom branded gift projects.

Does Sendora Gift sell fixed retail gift boxes?

No. The site presents custom corporate gifting directions and reference solutions. Final product combinations are confirmed around buyer requirements.

What information is needed for a quote?

Buyers should share product direction, quantity, target budget if available, logo file, branding method, packaging needs, delivery country, postal code, target date and sample requirement.

Can Sendora Gift help choose products?

Yes. Buyers can share the audience, occasion, budget direction and delivery needs, then Sendora Gift can suggest practical product combinations for review.

Does Sendora Gift publish fixed prices?

No. Price depends on selected products, quantity, logo method, packaging, sample needs and delivery destination.

Does Sendora Gift publish fixed MOQ?

No. MOQ depends on the product, material, color, logo method, packaging and supplier conditions for the selected items.

Can product combinations be changed?

Yes. Gift set concepts can be adjusted based on recipient type, budget, preferred products, branding style and packaging requirements.

What logo methods are available?

Common methods may include printing, laser engraving, embroidery, embossing, debossing, labels, stickers, insert cards and branded packaging, depending on the selected product.

Can packaging be customized?

Packaging can often be planned with gift boxes, mailers, sleeves, stickers, paper bags, insert trays or cards. Final options depend on the project details.

Can buyers approve samples before bulk production?

Yes. Sample options can be discussed after product direction, logo artwork and packaging requirements are clear.

What is checked during sample approval?

Sample approval may check product choice, logo placement, color, material, packaging layout, insert card text and any project-specific details.

Can Sendora Gift arrange shipping?

Shipping can be planned by reviewing destination, quantity, product size, packaging, target date and buyer receiving requirements.

Can orders ship to multiple countries?

Split or multi-country delivery can be discussed when buyers provide destination counts, address requirements and target dates.

Are website photos completed customer orders?

Photos and product examples should be treated as representative gift sets, reference solutions, product concepts or customization directions unless a page explicitly verifies a completed order.

Can distributors work with Sendora Gift?

Yes. Promotional product distributors and sourcing agents can use Sendora Gift for custom gift set planning, branding, packaging and delivery coordination.

Can Sendora Gift help with employee welcome kits?

Yes. Employee welcome kits can be planned with useful branded items, packaging, welcome cards and shipment requirements.

Can Sendora Gift help with client appreciation gifts?

Yes. Client gifts can be planned around presentation, product usefulness, message card, packaging and delivery needs.

Can Sendora Gift help with event giveaways?

Yes. Event giveaways can be planned around attendee use, packing, logo visibility, booth distribution and shipment schedule.

How should buyers choose a corporate gift supplier?

Buyers should compare product fit, communication clarity, sample process, logo review, packaging capability, shipping planning and willingness to confirm details instead of making unsupported claims.

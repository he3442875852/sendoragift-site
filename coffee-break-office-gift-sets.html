Coffee Break Office Gift Set FAQ

Can we choose different mug styles?

Yes. Buyers can choose ceramic mugs, travel mugs, stainless steel tumblers, coffee cups or other drinkware options.

Can the gift box and card be customized?

Yes. We can support custom gift boxes, sleeves, stickers, thank you cards, welcome cards and paper bags.

Can we replace sticky notes with another item?

Yes. Sticky notes can be replaced by notebook, pen, coaster, keychain, desk accessory, mouse pad or another office product.

Yes. This solution is suitable for corporate procurement, distributors, agencies, employee programs and event gifting projects.

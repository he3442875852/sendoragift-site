What branded gift sets are realistic at USD 10–15 per person?

For standard ready-made products, stock colors and practical logo methods, buyers can review compact two- to four-item directions such as a tumbler, notebook and pen; a notebook and metal pen presentation set; or a passport holder and luggage tag set. How Quantity Changes the Plan

100 Recipients

Planning often starts around 100 sets for standard gift-set projects. Use stock colors, simple logo positions and an existing box structure where possible. Final MOQ follows the highest practical requirement among the selected products and packaging.

300–500 Recipients

Larger programs can make setup and consistent packing easier to distribute across units, but special colors, new box structures or multiple recipient versions can add complexity. Confirm one approval owner and the location split early.

What the Target Can Cover

Clarify whether the target covers products only, products plus standard logo work, or products plus packaging. Separate freight until the destination, carton plan and route are reviewed.

Where to Protect Value

Choose one useful hero item, one or two supporting items and one clear brand touchpoint. Fewer useful items and compact packaging usually create a stronger result than overcrowding the box.

BUYER FAQ

Budget Questions Before Quoting

We have a budget of USD 10–15 per person for 100–500 employees. What branded gift set combinations are realistic?

Start with standard products, stock colors and compact combinations. Share quantity, recipient type, destination and whether packaging or freight must fit inside the target.

Does the USD 15 target include international shipping?

Not automatically. Freight is reviewed after destination, postal code, carton size, quantity and target date are known.

Can the products and packaging both carry our logo?

Yes, subject to the selected surfaces, artwork and quantity. A main product logo plus a branded sleeve, card or box mark can control cost.

Can Sendora Gift recommend alternatives if one item exceeds the target?

Yes. The product mix, material, logo position or packaging direction can be adjusted after the budget driver is identified.

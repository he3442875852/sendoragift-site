Custom Corporate Gift Production Process

A practical workflow for B2B gift projects from requirement review to product confirmation, logo artwork, sample approval, production, inspection, packing and shipping.

Step-by-Step Project Workflow

1

Requirement Review

Buyer provides recipient type, product direction, quantity, budget, destination and timeline. A project cannot move clearly without these basics.

2

Product and Material Confirmation

Product model, material, color, size and packaging direction are reviewed before quotation or sampling.

3

Logo Artwork Review

Vector artwork, logo size, placement and color expectations should be checked against product surfaces.

4

Mockup or Sample Discussion

Digital mockups or samples may be discussed depending on the product, logo method, quantity and buyer approval needs.

5

Sample Approval Where Required

Sample approval may confirm product quality, material feel, logo effect, packaging fit and insert-card layout.

6

Bulk Production

Bulk production follows approved product, artwork, quantity and packaging details. Changes at this stage may affect timing.

7

Quality Inspection

Key product, logo, quantity and packing details are checked against confirmed requirements.

8

Packing

Gift boxes, inserts, sleeves, bags, carton labels and packing methods are prepared according to the approved plan.

9

Shipping Arrangement

Shipping options are reviewed after destination, packed carton details and requested timeline are clear.

How Buyers Can Avoid Production Delays

  • Supply vector artwork and brand color references where available.
  • Confirm logo size, position and branding method early.
  • Approve product color, material and packaging before bulk production.
  • Confirm final quantity, delivery destination and target date.
  • Use logo method guidance, quality control and shipping planning before sending a final RFQ.

FAQ

What can delay a custom corporate gift project?

Missing logo artwork, unconfirmed colors, changing quantities, late packaging approval and incomplete delivery details can delay quotation, sampling or production.

Is a sample always required before bulk production?

Not always. Sample needs depend on the selected product, logo method, packaging complexity, quantity and buyer approval requirements.

What must be approved before bulk production starts?

Product model, material, color, logo size, logo position, branding method, quantity, packaging and destination details should be confirmed before production proceeds.

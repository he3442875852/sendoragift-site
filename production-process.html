How a Project Moves Forward

A structured process helps procurement teams control timing, budget, branding details and shipping expectations.

1

2

Recommendation

3

Quotation

FAQ

How long does production take?

Timing depends on product type, quantity, logo method and packaging. Simple items can be faster than fully customized gift boxes.

Can you help choose products?

Yes. We can recommend product combinations based on recipient, budget and campaign purpose.

Can the process support repeat orders?

Yes. Confirmed product details and packaging notes can help make repeat projects easier.

Typical Corporate Gift Production Timeline

Production time depends on product complexity, logo method, packaging and quantity. A clear timeline helps buyers plan launch events, onboarding dates and holiday delivery with fewer last-minute changes.

1. Product Confirmation

Confirm item list, budget, quantity, colors, logo files, packaging direction and delivery destination before sampling.

2. Sample Approval

Samples help verify product quality, logo placement, packaging size and the overall unboxing experience before mass production.

3. Bulk Production

After approval, products, branding, packaging and final inspection are arranged according to the confirmed project details.

Information That Speeds Up Production

  • Vector logo files, Pantone colors and preferred logo size.
  • Final quantity, item combinations, recipient type and deadline.
  • Packaging artwork, card text, carton label needs and shipping address.

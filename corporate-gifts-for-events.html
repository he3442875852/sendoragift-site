Event Gift Planning by Distribution Point

Event gifts should be easy to transport, quick to hand out and useful after the event. The product choice depends on whether gifts are placed at seats, handed out at registration, packed in attendee bags or shipped after the event.

Registration Gifts

Lanyards, tote bags, notebooks, pens and bottles help attendees carry materials and remember the event brand.

VIP Guest Gifts

Custom boxes, premium drinkware, notebooks and greeting cards are suitable for speakers, sponsors and executive guests.

Post-Event Gifts

Follow-up gift boxes can support customer appreciation, sales meetings and partner relationship building after the event.

Event Order Details to Prepare

  • Event date, delivery address, setup date and required carton labels.
  • Artwork for event logo, sponsor logos, badge colors or campaign message.
  • Expected attendee quantity plus extra stock for staff, speakers and replacements.

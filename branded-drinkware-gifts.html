Yes, drinkware can be packed with notebooks, pens, cards, bags or other items when the box size, protection and shipping method are confirmed.

Can drinkware samples be reviewed before bulk orders?

Yes, drinkware samples can be used to review product quality, logo effect, color, lid style and packaging fit before bulk production.

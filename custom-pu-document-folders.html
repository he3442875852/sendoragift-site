PRODUCT SELECTION

Choose the Structure Before Comparing Prices

“PU folder” can describe several products. A clear reference prevents suppliers from quoting different structures under the same name.

Opening Document Folder

Suitable for presentations, meetings and certificates when papers need to stay flat and organized. Confirm inner pockets, pen holder, card slot, closure and paper capacity only when required.

Envelope-Style File Pouch

A slim option for carrying papers, onboarding documents or conference materials. Confirm horizontal or vertical opening, flap shape and snap, zipper or magnetic closure where applicable.

Presentation Portfolio

Useful for client meetings, sales teams, training and formal document handover. Select the standard model first, then review whether a notebook, pen or business card holder should be quoted separately.

Stock Colors First

Black, navy, brown, grey and other available neutral colors are practical starting points. Special color matching can change MOQ, sample steps, cost and timing.

CUSTOMIZATION

Logo, Sample, Packaging and Shipping Decisions

Logo Method

Debossing can create a subtle tone-on-tone effect. Screen printing, foil stamping or color printing may suit artwork that needs stronger visibility. The selected PU surface and real logo detail determine the practical method.

MOQ Planning

Many standard stock-color projects can be reviewed from around 100 pieces. Final MOQ follows the exact folder model, color, decoration, packaging and current availability rather than one universal number.

Sample Approval

Review external dimensions, document fit, stitching, edge finish, closure, logo size, logo position and packing. Written approval should identify the exact model and approved artwork version.

Packing and Delivery

Folders can use individual bags, sleeves, paper bags or gift boxes. Because rigid packaging can add volume, provide the delivery country, postal code, target date and whether products ship alone or as part of a set.

Buyer FAQ

What is the difference between a PU document folder and a PU file pouch?

A document folder usually opens to organize papers and may include pockets or holders. A file pouch or envelope is a slimmer closed format for carrying documents. The final structure should be confirmed from a product photo or sample before quotation.

Which sizes are commonly requested for PU folders?

A4 and A5 are common planning sizes for corporate document folders and pouches. Exact external dimensions, paper capacity, opening direction and closure must be confirmed for the selected model.

How can a logo be added to a PU folder?

Depending on the PU surface and artwork, options may include debossing, screen printing, foil stamping or color printing. The suitable method is confirmed after the product surface, logo detail, position and quantity are reviewed.

What is the MOQ for custom PU document folders?

Many standard stock-color projects can be planned from around 100 pieces, but final MOQ depends on the selected model, color, logo method, packaging and availability.

Can buyers approve a sample before bulk production?

Yes. A sample or sample photo can be used to confirm folder size, color, stitching, closure, logo position, logo effect and packaging before bulk production.

Can PU folders be packed in a corporate gift box?

Yes, if the selected folder dimensions and the other items fit a practical box structure. Flat individual packing, a sleeve, a paper bag or a fitted gift box can be reviewed according to presentation and shipping needs.

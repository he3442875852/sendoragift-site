Corporate Gift FAQ

What types of corporate gift sets can Sendora Gift support?

We help buyers plan branded notebooks, drinkware, pens, bags, apparel, packaging and mixed corporate gift sets based on recipient group, budget and delivery needs.

Can you add our logo to products and packaging?

Yes. Logo feasibility depends on material, surface, color count, artwork size and order quantity. We confirm decoration options during sampling or quotation.

Can you help with sample references before bulk production?

Yes. Sample references and showroom photos can guide planning, but final materials, colors, logo placement and packaging should be confirmed before production.

Do you provide custom gift box packaging?

Yes. We can discuss box structure, insert layout, logo position, color and protective packing based on the selected product mix.

What information should I send for a quotation?

Please share product type, quantity, logo artwork, target budget, delivery country, deadline and any reference photos or packaging requirements.

Can you ship internationally?

We can help plan international delivery requirements with the buyer. Shipping method, destination rules, carton size and timeline need to be confirmed for each order.

Do you publish certifications or customer reviews?

We only publish certifications, reviews or completed case results when supporting evidence is available. Unverified examples are labeled as sample references.

Corporate Gift FAQ

Answers to common questions about custom gifts, logo branding, MOQ, samples, production and delivery.

Frequently Asked Questions

Use these answers to prepare a clearer inquiry and reduce back-and-forth before quotation.

Can corporate gifts be customized with our logo?

Yes. Logo methods include printing, engraving, embroidery, UV printing, heat transfer, labels and custom packaging depending on product material and quantity.

What information is needed for a quote?

Product idea, quantity, logo file, packaging requirements, target budget, delivery country and target date help us recommend suitable options faster.

Can we confirm a sample before bulk production?

Yes. Samples are recommended for custom logo placement, packaging layout and premium gift sets.

What affects MOQ?

MOQ depends on product type, material, logo method, packaging requirements and production setup.

How long does production take?

Lead time depends on product complexity, order quantity, logo method and packaging. Event deadlines should be shared early.

Do you support custom packaging?

Yes. Options include gift boxes, sleeves, inserts, greeting cards, labels and branded paper bags.

Can you ship worldwide?

Yes. Shipping can be discussed by express, air, sea or door-to-door options depending on destination and timing.

Can you recommend products by budget?

Yes. We can prepare practical options for different budget levels, recipients and campaign goals.

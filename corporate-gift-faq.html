Corporate Gift FAQ for B2B Buyers

Short answers to common procurement questions about products, logo customization, MOQ, samples, packaging, approval, shipping and quote requirements.

Products and Gift Combinations

Can standard ready-made products be customized with our logo?

Yes. Standard ready-made products can often be customized with printing, engraving, embroidery, labels or packaging branding depending on material and artwork.

Does every product have the same MOQ?

No. MOQ depends on product type, material, logo method, packaging, quantity and whether custom colors or custom structures are required.

Can different products be combined into one gift set?

Yes. Drinkware, notebooks, pens, bags, cards and packaging can be combined when product sizes, MOQ and packing requirements work together.

Logo Customization

Which logo files should we provide?

Vector artwork such as AI, EPS or editable PDF is preferred. High-resolution PNG or JPG can be used for early review when vector files are not ready.

What logo methods are available?

Common methods include printing, laser engraving, embroidery, UV printing, heat transfer, embossing, debossing and packaging branding.

MOQ, Samples and Packaging

Can we request a sample before bulk production?

Sample options depend on product selection, logo method and packaging. Samples or sample photos can help confirm key details before bulk production.

Can the gift box also be customized?

Yes. Boxes, sleeves, paper bags, labels, insert cards and carton marks can be discussed based on quantity, artwork and packaging structure.

Production, Shipping and Quote Requirements

Can you help plan gifts for employees, clients or events?

Yes. We can help route buyers toward employee welcome kits, client appreciation gifts, conference gifts, trade show giveaways or bulk corporate gifts.

What details are required for an accurate quote?

Share product direction, quantity, target budget, logo file, packaging needs, delivery country, postal code, target date and sample requirements.

Can gifts be shipped to an office, event venue or freight forwarder?

Shipping can be planned for offices, venues, warehouses or freight forwarders when destination details, receiving rules and packed carton information are confirmed.

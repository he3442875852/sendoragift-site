Planning Notes for ID Card Holders

Badge holder projects should start with card size, orientation and use environment. Employee badges, conference passes, student cards and visitor IDs may require different openings, attachment points and holder materials.

The card itself is not included unless specifically quoted. Buyers should clarify whether they need the holder only, holder plus lanyard, printed insert cards or a complete event badge kit.

Quote Inputs Buyers Should Prepare

  • Card size and vertical or horizontal direction
  • Material preference: PU, soft PVC or hard plastic
  • Holder only or paired with lanyard
  • Logo method and logo position
  • Packing method and delivery schedule

Materials, Formats and Buying Use Cases

PU Badge Holders

PU badge holders are often selected for office, VIP, training or corporate travel programs where a softer business look is preferred. Logo options may include printing, embossing or foil stamping depending on the surface.

Soft PVC Holders

Soft PVC holders can be practical for events, schools and visitor badges. Buyers should confirm transparency, card insertion direction, thickness and compatibility with clips or lanyards.

Hard Plastic Holders

Hard plastic holders are useful when a more rigid card protector is needed. Vertical and horizontal options should be matched to the card artwork, badge scanner direction and attachment point.

Programs That Use Badge Holders

Common applications include employee access, conference check-in, school IDs, training programs, visitor management, distributor event kits and branded onboarding packs.

Logo, MOQ, Sampling, Packaging and Shipping

Logo Customization

Logo options depend heavily on material. PU holders may support embossing, foil stamping or printing, while PVC and hard plastic holders may use printing or label-style branding. Logo position should not block card visibility.

Color and MOQ Factors

Stock colors and standard sizes are usually easier to quote. Many standard stock-color projects may start from approximately 100 pieces, but final MOQ depends on holder material, color, size, logo method and packaging.

Sample Process

Sample review should confirm card fit, opening direction, clip compatibility, logo placement, material feel and whether the card remains readable when inserted. If the buyer needs printed cards, that should be quoted separately.

Packing and Shipping Details

Badge holders can be bulk packed, individually bagged or paired with lanyards. International shipping quotes need destination country, postal code, carton requirements, delivery deadline and event venue constraints.

Buyer FAQ

Is the ID card included with the holder?

No. The card is not included unless it is specifically quoted. Buyers should say whether they need holders only, printed cards, lanyards or a complete badge kit.

Can holders be vertical or horizontal?

Yes. Vertical and horizontal options can be reviewed based on card artwork, badge scanner direction and attachment hardware.

Which materials are common?

PU, soft PVC and hard plastic are common choices. The best option depends on the use environment, budget, branding style and expected handling.

Can Sendora Gift confirm MOQ before sampling?

Yes. The team can review product direction, color, logo method and packaging first, then confirm the practical MOQ for that exact combination. Many standard stock-color projects may start from approximately 100 pieces, but final MOQ depends on the selected product, color, logo method and packaging.

Can buyers approve a sample before bulk production?

Yes. Buyers can request a sample, sample photo or pre-production confirmation depending on product type, timeline and logo method. Bulk production should start after the approved details are clear.

What information is needed for international shipping?

Please provide destination country, postal code, quantity, expected carton limits if any, target delivery date and whether the shipment should go to one address or multiple addresses.

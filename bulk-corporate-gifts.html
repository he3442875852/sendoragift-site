Buyer Questions

What is the typical MOQ for custom corporate gifts?

Typical MOQ depends on the selected products, branding method and packaging. Stock-color items may support lower quantities, while custom colors, molded items or special packaging may require higher minimums.

Can buyers use their own freight forwarder?

Yes, buyers can use their own freight forwarder when pickup, carton details, shipping marks and export handover requirements are confirmed before packing.

Why is sample approval important for bulk gifts?

Sample approval helps confirm product quality, logo position, color, packaging fit and card layout before bulk production starts, reducing avoidable mistakes on large orders.

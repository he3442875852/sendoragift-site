QUICK ANSWER

What does this physical sample confirm?

It confirms that a tumbler, pen and PU organizer were arranged together in a fitted black presentation box and that the same gold-color artwork was visibly repeated across the lid and individual items. The photo is useful for reviewing contrast, branding consistency and insert fit.

It does not confirm the exact decoration technique, materials specification, quantity, buyer approval, bulk production or delivery result.

Visible configuration

  • Matte black cylindrical tumbler
  • Black pen with a small coordinated mark
  • Black PU organizer with strap closure
  • Fitted black insert and rigid presentation box
  • Gold-color crest artwork repeated on four surfaces

SAMPLE REVIEW POINTS

What a buyer should check before approval

  • Whether the artwork remains legible at the smaller pen size
  • Whether the gold-color effect is consistent on different coatings
  • Whether the organizer strap and tumbler clear the insert edges
  • Whether the lid artwork is centered at the approved scale
  • Whether each product is protected during transit

The exact imprint method should be chosen after the real materials, coating, artwork detail and quantity are reviewed.

RELATED BUYER QUESTION

Can one logo effect work across a tumbler, pen, PU organizer and gift box?

A coordinated color direction can work, but the production method may differ by surface. A metal or coated tumbler, small pen barrel, PU cover and paper-wrapped box should each be tested for line detail, adhesion, contrast and scale. Approve the visible effect and the written specification for every item.

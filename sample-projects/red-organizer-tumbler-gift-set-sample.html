What a buyer should check before approval

  • Whether the red organizer matches the approved brand-color direction
  • Whether multicolor artwork is readable on the black tumbler and packaging
  • Whether the subtle organizer mark has enough contrast
  • Whether the box and bag artwork match in scale and orientation
  • Whether the insert protects the organizer edges and tumbler finish

RELATED BUYER QUESTION

Should a corporate gift set include both a rigid box and a paper bag?

A paper bag can help with hand presentation at meetings or events, while the rigid box supports the unboxing experience and product protection. The extra packaging adds cost and shipping volume, so buyers should confirm how recipients will receive the set before approving both layers.

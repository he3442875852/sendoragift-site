QUICK ANSWER

What does this mug sample confirm?

It confirms the visible balance of a white mug body, black accent surfaces and a black circular front mark on a physical item. Buyers can use the image to judge relative artwork scale, contrast and how a two-tone mug supports a monochrome logo direction.

It does not confirm mug capacity, ceramic grade, decoration process, dishwasher testing, order quantity or bulk production.

Visible configuration

  • White ceramic body
  • Black handle, rim and interior
  • One prominent circular front artwork
  • Black-on-white high-contrast presentation
  • Single product sample rather than a complete boxed set

SAMPLE REVIEW POINTS

What a buyer should check before approval

  • Artwork scale and distance from the mug base and rim
  • Line sharpness and filled areas in the circular mark
  • Black-tone consistency across handle, rim and interior
  • Print position when held by a right- or left-handed recipient
  • Protective packaging and drop-risk planning for bulk shipment

RELATED BUYER QUESTION

What should I approve on a custom logo mug before bulk production?

Approve the exact mug model, capacity and color; the artwork size, position and orientation; the decoration method; any color reference; and the protective packaging. If the mug will be combined with a coaster, spoon, coffee or card, also approve the final box fit.

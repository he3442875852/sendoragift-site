What does this physical sample confirm?

It confirms a coordinated navy-and-orange golf accessory direction inside a black presentation box, plus visible repeated artwork on several pieces. The video helps buyers review the unboxing sequence, item count, individual protective sleeves and close-range logo visibility.

It does not confirm exact textile materials, ball specification, accessory dimensions, decoration methods, quantity, buyer approval or bulk delivery.

RELATED BUYER QUESTION

What should buyers approve in a custom golf gift set sample?

Confirm the exact accessories and specifications, artwork scale on every item, thread or print colors where applicable, box insert layout, individual protection and any labeling required for the destination. Small pieces should be counted and secured so they do not move or disappear during transit.

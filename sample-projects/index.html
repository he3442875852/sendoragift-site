BUYER QUESTIONS

About These Sample Projects

Are these completed bulk customer orders?

No. They are physical sample-development records, not claims of bulk production, delivery or campaign results.

Why are the customer names not listed?

Customer identities and commercial details are withheld. Visible artwork remains the property of its respective owner.

Can I order the same configuration with my logo?

You can request a similar product and packaging direction using your own artwork. Availability, method, MOQ, cost and timing are reviewed for your specification.

Do the photos confirm the exact logo technique?

No. They confirm the visible sample effect. The exact production technique must be matched to the item material, coating, artwork and approved specification.

Product Category Entrances

Choose a category first, then confirm logo method, MOQ factors, sample requirements and packing details.

Lanyards and Badge Accessories

Custom logo lanyards and badge accessories are common for conferences, schools, employee programs and event registration. Polyester stock colors with screen printing or heat transfer are usually efficient starting points.

ID Card Holders

Custom ID card holders can include PU badge holders, soft PVC holders and hard plastic holders in vertical or horizontal formats. The card itself is not included unless specifically quoted.

PU Desk Accessories

PU desk mats, card holders and desktop accessories are practical for office gifts, employee kits and remote work programs where a useful daily item matters more than a decorative giveaway.

Travel Accessories

Custom luggage tags and passport holders support business travel programs, incentive trips and event travel kits. Logo options can include debossing, foil stamping or printing.

Custom Bags

Tote bags, drawstring bags, backpacks and document bags can carry event kits, onboarding items or promotional sets. Logo method depends on fabric, size and artwork detail.

Drinkware and Office Gifts

Drinkware, notebooks, pens, mugs and desk accessories are useful for corporate offices, HR teams and client programs. They can be purchased as single items or combined into a gift set.

Promotional Event Items

Event giveaways should be easy to carry, quick to distribute and relevant to the audience. Lanyards, badge holders, bags, pens and compact office items are common choices.

Custom Gift Packaging

Packaging can include boxes, sleeves, paper bags, insert cards, stickers and carton marks. Packaging requirements affect MOQ, sample steps, cost and shipping volume.

How Sendora Gift Plans Logo Product Orders

Standard Products First

For many corporate gift projects, Sendora Gift recommends standard ready-made products customized with a logo. This keeps sourcing clearer, makes samples easier to confirm and avoids overpromising new structures or special features that are not part of the selected product.

MOQ Depends on the Whole Specification

MOQ is confirmed by product, stock color, logo method, packing and schedule. A simple stock-color item can have a different MOQ from a special color, multi-position logo or custom package.

Sample Before Bulk Production

Buyers can confirm a sample or sample photo before mass production. The review should cover product color, logo size, logo position, packaging text, carton marks and any destination-specific packing notes.

Single Items or Gift Sets

A buyer can source one compact product or combine several items into a gift set. The best option depends on recipient type, event format, budget, destination and delivery timeline.

Buyer FAQ

What are custom logo products?

Custom logo products are standard or selected business items customized with a company logo, event mark, department name or campaign artwork. They can be purchased as single items or combined into corporate gift sets.

Which logo methods are available?

Common methods include screen printing, laser engraving, debossing, embossing, foil stamping, heat transfer and embroidery. The suitable method depends on material, surface shape, logo detail and quantity.

Can MOQ be confirmed before a quote?

MOQ can be discussed early, but final MOQ is confirmed after product type, color, logo method and packaging requirements are clear.

Can buyers approve samples first?

Yes. Sample confirmation is recommended before bulk production, especially when logo position, color matching or packaging text matters.

Can Sendora Gift arrange shipping?

Shipping options can be planned according to destination, quantity, carton size, schedule and whether the goods ship to one address or multiple addresses.

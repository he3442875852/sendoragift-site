Before ordering

Buyer questions

What are custom logo products?

Custom logo products are standard or selected business items customized with a company logo, event mark, department name or campaign artwork. They can be purchased as single items or combined into corporate gift sets.

Which logo methods are available?

Common methods include screen printing, laser engraving, debossing, embossing, foil stamping, heat transfer and embroidery. The suitable method depends on material, surface shape, logo detail and quantity.

Can MOQ be confirmed before a quote?

MOQ can be discussed early, but final MOQ is confirmed after product type, color, logo method and packaging requirements are clear.

Can buyers approve samples first?

Yes. Sample confirmation is recommended before bulk production, especially when logo position, color matching or packaging text matters.

Can Sendora Gift arrange shipping?

Shipping options can be planned according to destination, quantity, carton size, schedule and whether the goods ship to one address or multiple addresses.

Ready to narrow down an item?

Send the product category or photo reference, quantity, artwork, destination and target date. Final MOQ and sample timing depend on the complete specification.

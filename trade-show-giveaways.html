Buyer Questions

What logo branding methods are available for trade show giveaways?

Logo methods can include printing, UV printing, laser engraving, embroidery, heat transfer, labels and branded packaging, depending on the item material and artwork.

How is international shipping planned for exhibitions?

International shipping is planned from destination, venue or warehouse address, postal code, carton volume, target arrival date, customs needs and the event setup schedule.

Can different giveaway tiers be planned for one event?

Yes, teams can plan simple mass giveaways for booth visitors and more considered kits for scheduled meetings, VIP leads, speakers or partners.

Giveaway Types by Booth Objective

Trade show buyers often need two or three gift levels: high-volume handouts for general traffic, practical items for scanned leads, and selected visitor gifts for meetings or distributor conversations.

For broader marketing campaigns outside an exhibition booth, compare bulk promotional giveaways. This page focuses on booth storage, handout speed, lead-follow-up inserts and venue delivery planning.

Booth Giveaway Planning Details

High-Volume Handouts

Pens, lanyards, badge holders, keychains, stickers and compact desk items can work when visitors need to pick up the item quickly and keep moving.

Selected Visitor Gifts

Tote bags, bottles, notebooks, small kits or QR insert-card packs can be reserved for qualified leads, appointments, media contacts or distributor meetings.

Transport and Booth Packing

Lightweight products, master-carton labels and simple booth distribution packs help reduce event handling friction. Avoid bulky packaging unless it is for a smaller visitor group.

Exhibition Branding and Follow-Up Checklist

Logo Size and Placement

Choose visible logo areas for booth traffic, but keep artwork simple enough for the product surface. For detailed logo questions, review custom logo gifts.

QR Cards and Inserts

Insert cards can connect the giveaway to a campaign page, lead follow-up offer or meeting reminder without making the product itself too text-heavy.

Sample Review

Samples or sample photos help confirm logo effect, product color and packing method before bulk production starts.

Venue Delivery Details

Share event city, venue or warehouse address, booth schedule, arrival deadline and carton labeling requirements so shipping options can be reviewed.

I need 500 trade show giveaways—what should I give general visitors, qualified leads and VIP meetings?

General Visitors

Use lightweight, quick-handout items such as pens, lanyards, badge holders, compact notebooks or insert cards. Prioritize clear logo visibility and easy booth storage.

Qualified Leads

Reserve a tote, notebook and pen combination, a bottle or a compact follow-up kit for visitors who scan, book a meeting or match the target account profile.

VIP Meetings

Use fewer, more considered gifts with a message card and better presentation for scheduled meetings, speakers, partners or distributors.

Confirm the intended quantity for each tier before quotation so the product mix, packing labels and venue-delivery plan match the booth workflow.

Frequently Asked Questions

What trade show giveaways work for high-volume booth traffic?

Lightweight, useful and easy-to-brand items such as pens, notebooks, tote bags, lanyards, badge holders, bottles and small desk items are practical for booth traffic.

Can you plan different gifts for general visitors and qualified leads?

Yes. Many exhibition teams use simple handouts for general visitors and more considered kits or insert-card gifts for qualified prospects or scheduled meetings.

What should we share for exhibition delivery planning?

Share the event city, venue or warehouse address, booth schedule, target arrival date, quantity, packing preference and logo artwork so delivery options can be reviewed.

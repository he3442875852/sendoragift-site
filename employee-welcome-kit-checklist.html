Employee Welcome Kit Checklist

Plan useful, repeatable and branded onboarding kits for new hires and remote teams.

Request Welcome Kit Ideas

A good employee welcome kit helps new hires feel prepared and connected from day one. Use this checklist to plan product selection, branding, packaging and delivery before ordering.

Core Product Checklist

  • Drinkware: tumbler, mug, sports bottle or travel cup
  • Office basics: notebook, pen, folder, mouse pad or desk mat
  • Bags: tote bag, backpack or drawstring bag
  • Apparel: T-shirt, hoodie, cap or badge where suitable
  • Welcome materials: greeting card, culture card or insert card
  • Packaging: custom box, mailer box, paper bag, sleeve or label

Branding Checklist

  • Confirm logo file, brand colors and approved usage
  • Choose logo method for each product: printing, engraving, embroidery or embossing
  • Review logo size and placement before bulk production
  • Keep packaging design consistent with the company brand

Ordering Checklist

  • Confirm quantity and reorder plan
  • Set budget per kit, including packaging and shipping
  • Approve samples before production
  • Leave enough time for production and delivery
  • Prepare destination information for office or remote delivery
Buyer tip: If you hire regularly, choose stable products that can be reordered in future onboarding batches.

Employee Welcome Kit Checklist by Step

A welcome kit should make the first day easier and help new hires feel included. Planning the kit by step helps HR, procurement and brand teams avoid missing details.

Product List

Choose everyday items such as drinkware, notebooks, pens, bags, desk accessories, apparel, ID holders and welcome cards.

Brand Details

Prepare logo files, colors, card copy, packaging design, employee message and any department-specific inserts.

Delivery Plan

Decide whether kits ship to one office, multiple branches or individual remote employees, then confirm labels and deadlines.

Common Welcome Kit Combinations

  • Starter kit: notebook, pen, mug and welcome card.
  • Remote kit: tumbler, tote bag, desk mat, card and protective gift box.
  • Premium kit: backpack, insulated bottle, notebook, apparel and branded packaging.

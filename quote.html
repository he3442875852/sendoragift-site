How We Prepare a More Accurate Quote

1. Product Direction

We compare your preferred items with the recipient, use case, quantity and target budget so the recommended set is realistic for bulk production.

2. Branding Method

Logo printing, engraving, embroidery and packaging labels are quoted based on material, artwork color, logo size and order quantity.

3. Packaging and Shipping

Gift box structure, carton packing, delivery country and deadline can affect both unit price and freight planning, especially for bulky sets.

Before You Submit

If your project has a fixed event date, holiday deadline or multi-office delivery plan, include that timing in the message. Production method, packaging choice and shipping route should be planned together so the quote reflects the real schedule.

For logo work, vector artwork is preferred. If you only have a screenshot or rough reference, send it anyway and we can review whether it is enough for an initial recommendation.

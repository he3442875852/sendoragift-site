What does each type of sample confirm?

A digital mockup helps check layout and logo position. A physical product sample helps assess size and feel; a branded sample checks the decoration; a packed set checks fit and presentation. Agree which reference will govern production and record approvals for product, logo and packaging in writing.

What sample approval is meant to confirm

A corporate gift sample is not only a product preview. It is a decision checkpoint for the entire project. Buyers use it to confirm whether the chosen items match the audience, whether the logo position feels right, whether packaging supports the message and whether the team is ready to approve bulk work. This is especially important for gift sets that combine several products in one box.

Sendora Gift can discuss digital mockups, product samples, logo samples or packaging samples depending on the project. Not every order requires the same approval path. A simple repeat product may need fewer checks than a new combination with custom packaging and multiple decoration methods. The best approach is chosen after the buyer confirms product direction, quantity, artwork and deadline.

Digital mockup or physical sample: what can you check?

Choose the approval method by the decisions still open. Seeing an unbranded sample does not approve the logo, and approving a logo image does not confirm packaging fit.

Keep these six items in the written approval

  1. Product model, material, dimensions and color reference.
  2. Artwork version, logo dimensions, placement and method for every branded item.
  3. Accepted sample photos or the physical sample reference.
  4. Box dimensions, insert layout, protection and card wording.
  5. Required corrections, revision status and the named final approver.
  6. The agreed inspection points and any differences allowed in the final specification.

Compare these checks with the pre-shipment inspection checklist. Keep sample approval and final shipment inspection as separate checkpoints.

Moving from approval to bulk production

Bulk work should begin only after the agreed approval points are confirmed. That may include product list, logo file, imprint method, packaging design, card text, quantity and shipping destination. Inspection photos before shipment can help buyers review carton packing and visible product details, but they are not a substitute for early sample approval.

Timing depends on product selection, logo method, packaging complexity and revision cycles. Because each project is different, Sendora Gift confirms sample and bulk timing after the requirements are known. Buyers can protect the schedule by preparing artwork early and avoiding late changes after approval.

Decision notes for approval teams

Sample approval works best when the buyer writes down the decision criteria before the sample arrives. The team may need to check logo size, product color, box fit, card wording, recipient experience or shipment practicality. Without criteria, feedback can become subjective and difficult to act on.

For multi-stakeholder projects, one person should consolidate comments and confirm which changes are required. Late changes after approval can affect timing, packaging and cost. Sendora Gift can respond more clearly when feedback identifies the exact product, logo position or packaging element that needs adjustment.

Buyers should keep the approved sample notes with the quote record, because those notes become the reference for bulk production review, packaging confirmation and final inspection photo checks.

Questions before you order

Answers for Buyers

Is a digital mockup the same as a production sample?

No. A digital mockup helps review visual direction, artwork and placement. It does not establish physical color, texture, print adhesion, engraving contrast or packaging fit. Confirm which physical checks your order requires before releasing production.

What should I check on a branded sample?

Check the exact item, material, color, logo dimensions, placement, method and finish. Review small lettering at actual size. If the item goes in a gift set, also confirm how it fits with the box, insert and other products.

Are custom samples free?

Sample cost and timing are confirmed for the product and customization. Ask whether the quote includes artwork setup, decoration, packaging and sample delivery. Any credit against a later order must be agreed in writing; it should not be assumed.

Should I approve the box and card separately?

Yes, record the packaging and printed-copy approvals alongside product approval. Check box dimensions, insert fit, logo placement and the exact card wording. A product approval does not automatically approve a later box or card revision.

How should several departments provide feedback?

Appoint one approval owner to combine comments from procurement, brand and the recipient team. Identify each requested change by product or packaging element, artwork version and dimension. Send one final written decision so contradictory feedback does not reach production.

Can I reuse the sample reference for a repeat order?

Yes, keep the approved specification, artwork and sample reference as the comparison point. Before reordering, confirm the current model, material, color and decoration method. If an item or component has changed, agree what needs fresh approval.

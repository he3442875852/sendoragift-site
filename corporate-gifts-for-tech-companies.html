How to Plan Tech Company Gift Sets

Tech buyers usually need gifts that work across office teams, remote employees, developers, sales teams and partner communities. A strong tech gift set should be useful on a desk, easy to ship internationally and consistent with the brand's visual identity.

For Onboarding

Combine a laptop-friendly backpack or tote bag with a tumbler, notebook, pen, welcome card and branded box. This gives new hires practical items for their first week while reinforcing company culture.

For Product Launches

Choose lightweight items such as mugs, desk mats, stickers, caps and notebooks. Add campaign colors or product messaging on the insert card instead of overloading every item with text.

For Remote Teams

Use durable packaging, compact products and clear recipient labeling. Remote kits often work best with drinkware, notebooks, small tech accessories and personalized welcome cards.

Tech Gift Buying Checklist

  • Confirm whether gifts are for employees, prospects, developers, partners or VIP clients.
  • Prepare brand colors, logo files and preferred logo placement before sampling.
  • Use subtle branding for premium client gifts and stronger campaign branding for events.
  • Plan extra time for international shipping, especially when kits include multiple SKUs.

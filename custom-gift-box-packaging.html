Specify the complete set

What decides the box size and quote?

Begin with the final item list

Record each item’s dimensions and weight, its orientation in the box and any original protective pack. Decide what the recipient should see first and how each item will be removed.

Interior and exterior are different measurements. Confirm usable internal space for the items and the closed external size for packing and freight. Exact box dimensions are agreed after the product layout is reviewed.

Send a complete specification

  • Items, dimensions, weight and total set quantity.
  • Preferred format, insert or divider and the parts packed separately.
  • Logo positions, artwork version, finish reference and card text.
  • Assembly location: packed here or assembled at destination.
  • One address or multiple recipients, country, postal code and target arrival date.
  • Approval scope: fit sample, artwork proof or finished loaded sample.

Packaging questions

Before you request a quote

Can packaging be ordered without products?

Packaging-only projects can be discussed, but box size, material, quantity and delivery requirements need to be confirmed first.

What should we prepare for packaging quotation?

Send product dimensions, quantity, logo artwork, preferred box type, delivery country and whether cards or paper bags are needed.

Does every project need rigid boxes?

No. Rigid boxes work well for premium gifts, while paper boxes, mailers, sleeves or bags may be better for events and larger-volume programs.

What packaging options are available?

Packaging options include rigid boxes, paper boxes, mailer boxes, sleeves, labels, insert cards, dividers, tissue, paper bags and carton packing.

What should buyers confirm before custom packaging?

Buyers should confirm product combination, dimensions, quantity, logo file, brand colors, insert needs, budget, delivery method and sample requirement before finalizing packaging.

Can packaging be sampled before bulk production?

Yes, packaging samples or sample photos can be used to review box size, logo effect, insert layout and product fit before bulk packing.

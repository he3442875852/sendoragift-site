Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 15, 2026

Sendora Gift uses inquiry information to respond to corporate gift requests, prepare recommendations and communicate about custom gift projects.

Information We May Collect

  • Name, company name and contact details you submit
  • Project information such as quantity, budget, logo needs and delivery country
  • Files you choose to send, such as logo artwork or reference images

How We Use Information

We use submitted information to answer inquiries, prepare quotations, recommend products, discuss samples, arrange production details and coordinate delivery options.

Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Project details may be shared with relevant production or logistics partners only when needed to support your requested corporate gift project.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect inquiry information, but no online transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at rita@mcpatch.com.

File Uploads and Project Details

If you upload a logo file, design file or reference image, we use it only to review customization options, prepare artwork advice and discuss your requested corporate gift project. Uploaded materials may be shared with relevant production partners when needed to confirm logo method, sample feasibility or packaging layout.

Communication and Follow-Up

When you submit an inquiry, we may reply by email, WhatsApp or another contact method you provided. Follow-up communication is used to clarify product selection, MOQ, sample timing, quotation details, delivery destination and project status.

Retention

Inquiry records may be kept for reasonable business reference, repeat order support, quotation history and customer service. You may contact us if you want to update or request removal of submitted inquiry information where applicable.

International Inquiries

Sendora Gift works with B2B buyers in different countries. When an inquiry includes a delivery country, city or deadline, we use that information to discuss suitable shipping options, carton packing and estimated project timing. We may also use project history to support repeat quotations or related follow-up questions from the same company.

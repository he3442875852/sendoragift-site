How to Plan Corporate Holiday Gift Sets

Holiday gift sets should feel warm, useful and easy to present. Popular choices include mugs, tumblers, notebooks, blankets, candles, snack packaging, greeting cards and seasonal gift boxes.

For Christmas, New Year and annual appreciation programs, early planning helps confirm samples, packaging, production and shipping before peak season. Sendora Gift can help create flexible gift sets for clients and employees.

Frequently Asked Questions

What items can be included in a holiday gift set?

Popular items include ceramic mugs, tumblers, notebooks, pens, snacks, greeting cards, tote bags, blankets, candles, keychains, gift boxes and paper bags.

Can we customize holiday gifts with our company logo?

Yes. We support logo printing, engraving, embroidery, heat transfer, UV printing, custom greeting cards and seasonal packaging design.

Can you create Christmas or New Year packaging?

Yes. We can customize gift boxes, sleeves, paper bags, ribbons, stickers and insert cards with festive colors and seasonal messages.

What is the MOQ for holiday gift sets?

Most holiday gift set orders start from 100 units, depending on product selection, packaging style and customization method.

When should we start preparing holiday gift orders?

We recommend starting 6– weeks before your target delivery date, especially for Christmas, New Year and large corporate gifting projects.

Do you provide worldwide shipping?

Yes. We support international shipping by express, air, sea or door-to-door logistics depending on your schedule and budget.

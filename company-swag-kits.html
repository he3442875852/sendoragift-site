Popular Product Mixes

  • Premium team box: backpack, insulated bottle, apparel item and rigid box

Branding and Packaging

  • Use consistent logo size and color across items. For shipping, consider mailer boxes, sleeves, labels, cards and carton protection. For event handouts, bags and simple inserts may be more practical.

Reordering Advice

  • If the kit will be reused for onboarding, choose stable product types and keep artwork organized so future repeat orders are faster to confirm.

Swag Kit Planning for Employees and Campaigns

Swag kits perform best when each version has a clear recipient, distribution method and reorder plan.

New Hire Swag Kits

New hire swag kits usually include a mug or bottle, notebook, pen, tote bag and welcome card. Keep the product list stable if HR will reorder the kit every month or quarter.

Remote Employee Swag Boxes

Remote employee swag boxes need protective packaging and clear address handling. Mailer boxes, cards and compact desk items are often easier to ship than oversized products.

Conference Swag Kits

Conference swag kits should be easy to carry and distribute. Tote bags, bottles, notebooks, pens and lanyards work well when logo visibility matters.

Customer Campaign Swag Kits

Customer campaign swag kits can combine a useful branded item with a card and simple packaging. The goal is recall and practical use, not a heavy gift box.

FAQ

What is usually included in a company swag kit?

Common items include drinkware, notebooks, pens, tote bags, apparel, desk items, cards and custom packaging.

Can swag kits be mailed to remote employees?

Yes. Packaging can be planned for bulk shipment or remote employee distribution depending on destination and packing requirements.

How should we choose between swag and premium gifts?

Swag kits work best for visibility and repeated use. Premium gifts are better when recipient level and presentation matter more than broad distribution.

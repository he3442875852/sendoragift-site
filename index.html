BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

A Few Useful Answers

What are custom corporate gift sets?

Custom corporate gift sets are coordinated product combinations prepared for business recipients. They can include drinkware, notebooks, bags, cards, packaging and logo branding, with final details confirmed by quantity, budget, artwork and delivery destination.

What information is needed for a corporate gift quote?

A clear quote needs quantity, target budget, product direction, logo file, branding method, packaging requirement, delivery country, postal code, target date and sample requirement. These details help confirm realistic MOQ, pricing and timing.

Can buyers change the items in a gift set?

Yes, buyers can change the items in a gift set. The final mix depends on recipient type, budget, product availability, branding method, packaging fit and delivery plan.

What is a typical MOQ for custom corporate gift sets?

For many standard custom gift-set projects, planning often starts at around 100 sets. Final MOQ depends on the selected products, stock colors, logo method and packaging structure.

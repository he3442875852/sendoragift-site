FAQ

Common Questions Before a Brief

What is the typical minimum order quantity?

Typical stock-color projects may start from around 100 sets, while fully customized colors or packaging may require a higher quantity. Final MOQ depends on the selected products and branding method.

Can I choose different items for my gift set?

Yes. You can start from one of our gift concepts or create a custom combination based on your audience, budget and occasion.

Can you make samples before bulk production?

Yes. Sample timing and cost are confirmed after the products, logo artwork and packaging direction are selected.

What branding methods are available?

Common options include printing, laser engraving, embroidery, embossing, labels, custom cards and branded packaging.

Can you arrange international shipping?

Shipping can be planned by express, air, sea or door-to-door service depending on destination, quantity and deadline.

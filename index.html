Why Choose Sendora Gift

Sendora Gift is a professional corporate gift supplier specializing in custom gift sets, employee welcome kits, promotional giveaways, executive gift boxes, conference gifts, sports event kits and holiday gift solutions for global B2B clients.

We provide one-stop OEM and ODM services including product sourcing, logo customization, packaging design, sample support, quality inspection and worldwide shipping. Our goal is to help companies create meaningful branded gifts that improve customer relationships, employee engagement and event experiences.

Whether you are preparing a new employee onboarding program, a trade show giveaway campaign, a VIP client appreciation project or a seasonal corporate gift plan, our team can help you build a flexible gift set based on your brand, budget and delivery schedule.

About Sendora Gift

Sendora Gift helps companies create customized corporate gifts for employees, customers, events and brand campaigns. We work with B2B buyers, event agencies, distributors and procurement teams to build practical gift sets with logo branding, custom packaging and reliable delivery.

Our gift solutions cover drinkware, notebooks, bags, apparel, office accessories, event merchandise, premium gift boxes and seasonal gift sets. From product selection to packaging design and shipment, our team supports the complete corporate gifting process.

Quality Control

Before shipment, we check product appearance, logo position, printing effect, packaging condition and quantity accuracy. Sample orders, production photos and final inspection support are available for custom gift projects.

Customization Methods

Available branding methods include screen printing, UV printing, laser engraving, embroidery, heat transfer, embossing, custom labels, paper sleeves, greeting cards and branded gift boxes.

Sample & Production

Samples help you confirm product quality, logo effect and packaging before bulk production. Standard production time is usually 3-5 weeks depending on quantity, product complexity and customization details.

Shipping Support

We support express, air, sea and door-to-door logistics for corporate gift orders worldwide, helping buyers plan delivery for onboarding, conferences, campaigns and seasonal programs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is your minimum order quantity?

Most gift sets start from 100 units, but MOQ may vary depending on product type and customization method.

Can we customize products with our logo?

Yes. We support logo printing, laser engraving, embroidery, heat transfer, UV printing and custom packaging design.

Can I choose different items for one gift set?

Yes. Customers can choose different products such as tumblers, notebooks, bags, umbrellas, caps, pens and gift boxes to create their own set.

Do you provide sample orders?

Yes. Sample orders are available before bulk production so you can check product quality, logo effect and packaging.

How long does production take?

Production usually takes 3–5 weeks depending on quantity, product complexity and customization requirements.

Do you ship worldwide?

Yes. We provide worldwide shipping support by express, air, sea or door-to-door logistics depending on your needs.

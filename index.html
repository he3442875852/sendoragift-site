Frequently Asked Questions

What is your minimum order quantity?

Most gift sets start from 100 units, but MOQ may vary depending on product type and customization method.

Can we customize products with our logo?

Yes. We support logo printing, laser engraving, embroidery, heat transfer, UV printing and custom packaging design.

Can I choose different items for one gift set?

Yes. Customers can choose different products such as tumblers, notebooks, bags, umbrellas, caps, pens and gift boxes to create their own set.

Do you provide sample orders?

Yes. Sample orders are available before bulk production so you can check product quality, logo effect and packaging.

How long does production take?

Production usually takes 3–5 weeks depending on quantity, product complexity and customization requirements.

Do you ship worldwide?

Yes. We provide worldwide shipping support by express, air, sea or door-to-door logistics depending on your needs.

FAQ

Common Questions Before a Brief

What is the MOQ for custom corporate gift sets?

MOQ depends on the selected products, customization method, packaging structure and quantity. We confirm the suitable order quantity after reviewing your product direction and logo requirements.

Can I choose different items for my gift set?

Yes. You can start from one of our gift concepts or create a custom combination based on your audience, budget and occasion.

Can we approve samples before bulk production?

Yes. Sample timing and cost are confirmed after the products, logo artwork, branding method and packaging direction are selected.

What logo files and branding methods are useful?

Vector artwork is preferred when available. Common branding options include printing, laser engraving, embroidery, embossing, labels, custom cards and branded packaging.

Can packaging and shipping be planned together?

Yes. Gift box size, carton packing, destination, quantity and target date should be reviewed together before choosing a shipping method.

A useful brief

Four things that shape the recommendation

  1. Recipients & use

    Who receives the gift, and where will it be used or handed out?

  2. Quantity & budget

    Give a total and any variants. State whether the target budget includes packing and freight.

  3. Logo & sample

    Share the artwork and the sample details your team needs to approve.

  4. Destination & date

    Confirm one address or several, country, postal code and the required arrival date.

Gift-set planning often starts around 100 sets. Final MOQ, sample cost and timing depend on the selected items, colors, logo work and packing. Read the quantity guide.

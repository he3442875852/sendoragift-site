What to approve on a client gift box sample

Product and logo finish

Review the actual color, surface finish and logo position on every selected item. Ask which logo method suits the material and whether small artwork details remain readable.

Message and presentation

Confirm the card text, spelling, orientation and box-lid artwork. Check that the logo, card and products are visible in the intended opening order.

Fit and protective packing

Check the insert against the selected items, including mug handles and lids. Agree the box structure, outer carton and handling needs before confirming the shipping quote.

From your brief to an approved gift set

  1. Share the brief

    Send the recipient, quantity, preferred items, target budget and required destination or date. A short description is enough to start.

  2. Review the proposal

    Compare the selected items, logo methods, packaging, minimum quantity and a quote for the agreed delivery plan.

  3. Approve the sample

    Confirm the artwork, product finish, colors, message card and packaging fit. Sample cost and timing are agreed for the project.

  4. Confirm production and dispatch

    Agree the production specification, inspection points, carton packing and shipping arrangement before the bulk order proceeds.

Buyer questions

Can the box and thank-you card carry our branding?

Yes. Box, sleeve and card options depend on the selected structure, artwork and quantity. Confirm the card message, logo placement and print finish with the sample.

Can I change the items in the client appreciation box?

Yes. The combinations are starting points. A change in drinkware, stationery or accessories may affect the insert, box size, logo method and freight, so the complete combination is reviewed together.

Can regular and VIP versions share one theme?

Yes. Use the same card message or artwork style, then vary the main item, finish or box structure. Quote each version against its own quantity and approved specification.

What quantity and delivery time should I plan for?

Around 100 sets can be a starting point for planning, but the final minimum depends on items, stock colors, logos and packaging. Sample and production timing are confirmed for the selected combination and delivery destination.

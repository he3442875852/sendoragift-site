Sample Plan: 2,000 Trade Show Giveaways

Sample Project / Reference Solution

A high-quantity promotional gift project designed to attract booth visitors and keep brand exposure after the event.

Example Project Requirement

This planning example assumes affordable branded giveaways for a three-day exhibition. The items need to be lightweight, easy to hand out and useful enough for visitors to keep.

Giveaway Selection

Branded Pens

Low-cost, practical and easy to distribute in large quantities.

Tote Bags

Reusable bags with large logo area for walking brand exposure.

Lanyards

Useful during events and suitable for sponsor visibility.

Stickers and Cards

Simple printed materials to support campaign messaging.

Cost and Delivery Planning

Sendora Gift helped the buyer choose products with stable production, fast customization and efficient packing. Because trade show schedules are fixed, the project timeline included early artwork confirmation, production inspection and shipping buffer before the event date.

Planning Details

The exhibition team needed giveaways that could attract booth visitors without creating heavy shipping cartons or slow handout lines. The final product direction focused on practical items with clear logo visibility and easy distribution.

For this type of project, cost control and deadline control are both important. Artwork approval, quantity tiers, carton count and venue delivery rules were checked before production so the giveaway sets could arrive before booth setup.

Buyer Takeaway

For trade show giveaways, share booth visitor estimates, event date, venue city, preferred handout style and whether VIP lead gifts are needed separately.

Recommended Alternatives

Use pens, tote bags and lanyards for high-volume traffic. Use tumblers, notebooks or boxed sets for qualified leads and partners.

Planning Outcome

This reference direction shows how booth giveaway sets can support visitor conversations and brand visibility planning before final order details are confirmed.

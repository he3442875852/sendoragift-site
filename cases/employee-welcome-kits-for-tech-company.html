500 Employee Welcome Kits for a Technology Company

Sample Project / Reference Solution

A practical onboarding gift project built for new hires, remote employees and internal brand culture.

Project Requirement

The buyer needed 500 employee welcome kits for a technology team. The kit had to feel useful for daily office work, support custom logo branding, include a welcome message and be ready for phased delivery.

Gift Set Combination

Drinkware

Insulated stainless steel tumbler with laser engraved company logo.

Office Items

A5 notebook and metal pen for onboarding meetings and daily work.

Bag

Reusable tote bag with screen printed logo for internal events.

Packaging

Custom gift box, paper insert and welcome card with brand message.

Customization and Production

Sendora Gift recommended laser engraving for the tumbler, screen printing for the tote bag and a custom printed sleeve for the gift box. A pre-production sample helped confirm logo size, box layout and product color before bulk production.

Production was planned around a 3-5 week timeline after sample approval, with quality checks for logo position, packaging condition and quantity accuracy before shipment.

Planning Details

The HR team wanted onboarding kits that felt useful beyond the first day. The product mix needed to work for office employees and remote staff, so daily-use items such as drinkware, notebooks and tote bags were prioritized over novelty merchandise.

Brand consistency was checked across the welcome card, box, bag and individual products. Sample review focused on logo readability, color balance, box fit and whether each item could be packed cleanly for bulk delivery to the company office.

Buyer Takeaway

For welcome kits, prepare employee quantity, joining schedule, office or home delivery needs, brand guidelines and whether names or departments need personalization.

Recommended Alternatives

For tech teams, a laptop sleeve, cable pouch, insulated tumbler or desk mat can be added when the budget allows.

Planning Outcome

This reference direction shows how welcome kits can be planned for a consistent onboarding experience and adjusted for future hiring batches after final details are confirmed.

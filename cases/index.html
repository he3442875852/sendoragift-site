How to Use These Sample References

Each sample reference is for planning quantity, product mix, logo method, packaging and delivery. The final recommendation can be adjusted for your budget, brand color, destination country and deadline.

Compare by Quantity

Small VIP projects may focus on premium packaging and higher-value products, while larger event projects usually need lighter items, faster packing and stable unit costs.

Compare by Recipient

Employees, clients, conference attendees and trade show visitors respond to different gifts. A useful daily item may work better for employees, while client gifts often need a stronger unboxing experience.

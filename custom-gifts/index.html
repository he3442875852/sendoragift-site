Custom Gift FAQ

These questions cover the most common details buyers ask before sending artwork or requesting a quote.

What logo customization methods are available?

Common logo methods include screen printing, laser engraving, embroidery, UV printing, heat transfer, embossing, labels and custom packaging. The best method depends on material, quantity and budget.

Can gift packaging be customized?

Yes. Custom packaging options can include rigid gift boxes, sleeves, paper bags, insert cards, greeting cards, labels and branded cartons.

Can I confirm a sample before bulk production?

Yes. Sample confirmation is recommended for logo placement, color, product quality and packaging layout before bulk production.

What files should I send for logo customization?

Vector files such as AI, EPS, PDF or SVG are preferred. High-resolution PNG or JPG files can also be reviewed when vector artwork is not available.

Can you help if I only have a rough gift idea?

Yes. Share your recipient type, quantity, budget, delivery country and target date, and we can recommend product combinations and branding options.

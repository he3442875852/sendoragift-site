How We Support B2B Gift Projects

Corporate gift projects often involve several moving parts: product sourcing, artwork confirmation, sample approval, packaging layout, carton packing and international delivery. Sendora Gift helps buyers organize these steps so the final gift set is practical, brand-consistent and realistic for the deadline.

Clear Project Briefs

We help translate quantity, budget, recipient type and delivery country into a workable product direction before samples or bulk quotes are prepared.

Artwork and Sample Review

Logo size, color, placement and packaging details can be checked before production so buyers know what will be delivered.

For employee kits, events and client gift boxes, packing format matters. We consider set packing, carton labels, gift cards and shipment destination during planning.

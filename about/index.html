About Sendora Gift

We help companies build useful branded gifts for employees, clients, events and marketing campaigns.

Custom Corporate Gift Partner

Sendora Gift supports B2B buyers, procurement teams, agencies and distributors with custom corporate gift sets, employee welcome kits, promotional giveaways, event merchandise and premium gift boxes.

Our work covers product sourcing, logo customization, packaging design, sample support, production follow-up, quality inspection and worldwide shipping coordination.

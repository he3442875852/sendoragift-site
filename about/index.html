What We Are

Not just a product list. A project partner.

Corporate gifts usually fail when products, logo methods, packaging, timing and shipping are treated as separate decisions. Sendora Gift keeps those decisions connected from the first brief, so buyers can compare realistic options instead of chasing scattered quotes.

Our role is to make custom gifting easier to specify, easier to approve and easier to deliver. That includes product sourcing, decoration method advice, packaging structure, sample review, production follow-up and shipment planning.

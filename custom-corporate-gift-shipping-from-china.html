Shipping starts with product and packaging decisions

Corporate gift shipping from China is easiest to plan when product selection and packaging are discussed together. A rigid gift box may create a premium experience but also changes carton volume. A mug, bottle or fragile item may need stronger internal protection. A tote bag or flat notebook can pack very differently from a boxed executive set. These details affect the shipping method as much as the destination does.

Sendora Gift does not present one fixed delivery promise for every order. Instead, the shipment is reviewed after the product combination, quantity, packaging direction, destination and required date are clear. This approach is more useful for B2B buyers because it shows which details control cost, risk and timing.

Is DDP a shipping method?

DDP is a delivery term, not a transport mode. It can apply with different modes of transport and assigns substantial delivery and import responsibilities to the seller. Ask for the named destination and written confirmation of duties, taxes, handling and delivery scope. Availability must be checked for the specific destination and order. See the ICC explanation of DDP.

What to include in a freight inquiry

  • Product list, total quantity and quantity per delivery location.
  • Gift box dimensions and estimated carton count, size and gross weight when available.
  • Country, postal code, receiving address type and contact.
  • Required arrival date, receiving hours and appointment requirements.
  • Preferred forwarder and the delivery scope you want quoted.

How buyers can reduce shipping surprises

The best shipping inquiries include destination country, postal code, quantity, gift set concept, target date and whether the buyer has an import broker or preferred logistics partner. If the project requires individual addresses, that should be stated before packaging is finalized. If the buyer only needs bulk delivery to a warehouse, the carton plan can be simpler.

Confirm the final packing list and carton measurements against the approved order before booking transport. Sendora Gift can coordinate shipping planning, but buyers should confirm local import rules and internal receiving requirements where applicable.

Decision notes for shipment planning

Shipping is often treated as the final step, but for custom corporate gifts it can shape the entire project. A heavier bottle, a larger rigid box, a fragile mug or a multi-item kit may affect carton count and route discussion. Asking about shipment only after product approval can limit the practical options.

Buyers can reduce uncertainty by sharing the receiving plan early. One destination, multiple offices, distributor warehouse delivery and event venue delivery all need different information. Sendora Gift can coordinate around the confirmed order details, while buyers should check local import and receiving requirements on their side.

How do I compare shipping quotes for corporate gift sets?

Compare quotes for the same packed order, destination and receiving date. Check the carton count, dimensions, gross weight, transport method, estimated transit and included destination services. Confirm delivery terms and exclusions separately so that two prices cover the same scope.

Answers for Buyers

Should I choose express, air or sea freight for gift sets?

Compare them using the packed volume, weight, destination and required arrival date. Express is often reviewed for samples or smaller urgent shipments, air for time-sensitive consolidated orders, and sea for larger volume with more planning time. Confirm the actual route and delivery scope for the order.

Is DDP the same as sea shipping?

No. DDP describes delivery responsibilities and can apply with different transport modes. Sea freight describes how goods travel. Ask for the transport route, named destination and written scope of duties, taxes, handling and final delivery before comparing a DDP quote.

Why does a larger gift box change the freight quote?

A larger box can increase packed volume and the number of cartons even when the products stay the same. Ask for carton dimensions and gross weight for each packaging option. A compact layout must still protect the products adequately.

Can I use my own freight forwarder?

Yes, a buyer-appointed forwarder can be discussed. Provide the receiving or collection address, contact, carton marks, packing-list requirements and intended handover date. Confirm who handles each stage so the product quote and freight arrangements cover the same scope.

Can gift sets be shipped to several offices or countries?

Split delivery can be reviewed when you provide quantities per location, destination countries, postal codes and target dates. Feasibility, address requirements and costs must be confirmed for each shipment. Do not assume one bulk quote includes individual-recipient delivery.

What should I confirm for delivery to an event venue?

Confirm the venue’s receiving address, named contact, delivery window, booking or booth reference and any storage or appointment rules. Share the required arrival date before choosing the route. A successful dispatch does not by itself confirm acceptance at the venue.

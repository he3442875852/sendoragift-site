Written approval

Keep one reference from brief to shipment

  1. Define the scope

    Record items, quantity, branding, packing, destination and the evidence needed by your team.

  2. Approve the reference

    Agree what the proof or sample covers and record any open points before releasing production.

  3. Agree inspection

    List item, logo, quantity and packing checks and how exceptions will be handled.

  4. Confirm release

    Review the agreed records, carton details and delivery arrangement before shipment.

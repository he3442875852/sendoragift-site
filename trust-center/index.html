Sample Reference and Product Planning

Corporate gift buyers need to compare material, color, logo area, packaging fit and delivery feasibility before bulk production. Sendora Gift uses sample references and project briefs to narrow down a realistic product combination for each recipient group, quantity and target budget.

For buyer-provided showroom or sample photos, we recommend labeling them as sample references or project snapshots unless the customer name, order quantity, destination and final delivery result are confirmed.

How We Coordinate Supply

Sendora Gift works with selected suppliers for drinkware, stationery, bags, promotional products, metal accessories, apparel and custom packaging. We do not describe every product as coming from one single factory. Instead, we help buyers organize the right supplier combination for each project and manage artwork, samples, packaging and inspection details before shipment.

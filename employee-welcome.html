Prepare an onboarding brief your team can reuse

Define the core kit

List the items every new hire should receive, then separate optional items by role. Give the estimated quantity for each version so a bag or drinkware change does not alter the entire brief.

Approve one complete sample

Review the logo finish, card, item arrangement and closed box together. Check that the packed kit can be handled and stored in the way your HR team expects.

Buyer questions

What should be included in an employee welcome kit?

Useful work items and a welcome message are a good starting point. Common options include a notebook, pen, mug or tumbler, bag, lanyard, badge holder, mouse pad and branded packaging. Select items for the employee’s role and work location.

Can welcome kits be shipped to remote employees?

Individual delivery can be considered after checking destination coverage, postal codes, recipient-list handling and packaging protection. Confirm the shipping arrangement and cost before approving the order.

Can we reorder the same onboarding kit?

Use an approved specification for the items, colors, artwork and packaging as the basis of a repeat quote. Availability and any proposed substitutions need to be confirmed for each batch.

What is the minimum quantity for custom welcome kits?

Around 100 sets can be used for initial planning. The final minimum depends on the selected items, stock colors, logo methods and packaging. Share your hiring quantity so the combination can be reviewed.

Can we check a sample before bulk production?

Yes. Sample options, cost and timing are confirmed after product selection and artwork review. Check the product quality, logo finish, welcome card and packaging fit together before bulk approval.

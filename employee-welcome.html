How to Build Employee Welcome Kits

A strong employee welcome kit should feel useful from the first workday. Popular items include branded drinkware, notebooks, pens, tote bags, desk accessories, apparel and welcome cards.

For onboarding projects, buyers often choose durable daily-use products, consistent brand colors and packaging that presents the company culture clearly. Sendora Gift can help balance budget, MOQ, customization method and delivery schedule.

Frequently Asked Questions

What items are usually included in an employee welcome kit?

Popular items include backpacks, tumblers, notebooks, pens, ID holders, lanyards, tote bags, mugs, mouse pads, welcome cards and custom gift boxes.

Can we customize the welcome kit with our company logo?

Yes. We support logo printing, embroidery, engraving, UV printing, heat transfer and custom packaging design.

Can you create welcome kits for remote employees?

Yes. We can prepare bulk kits for office delivery or support individual shipment depending on your logistics requirements.

What is the MOQ for employee welcome kits?

Most welcome kit orders start from 100 units, depending on product selection and customization method.

Can I request a sample before mass production?

Yes. Sample orders are available so you can check product quality, logo effect and packaging before bulk production.

How long does production take?

Production usually takes 3–5 weeks depending on quantity, product complexity, packaging and logo customization.

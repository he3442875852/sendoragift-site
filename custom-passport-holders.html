Procurement Notes for Passport Holders

A standard PU passport cover is often practical when a buyer needs a professional travel accessory without complex development. The key decisions are material texture, color, internal pocket layout, logo method and packaging.

Buyers should confirm whether the passport holder is a single item, part of a travel accessory set or included in a broader corporate gift box.

Quote Inputs Buyers Should Prepare

  • Standard PU passport cover style
  • Logo method and logo position
  • Color preference and quantity
  • Packaging style or travel kit combination
  • Destination and target delivery date

Use Cases, Materials and Product Planning

Standard PU Passport Covers

Standard PU passport covers are common because they are compact, giftable and suitable for subtle branding. Buyers can compare texture, color, pocket layout and cover style before sample approval.

Corporate Travel Gifts

Passport holders are useful for executive trips, international sales meetings, employee travel programs, distributor incentives, study-abroad programs and conference welcome packs.

Common Color Direction

Black, navy, brown, grey and other restrained colors are typical for business travel gifts. Final stock color availability should be confirmed with the selected style.

Gift Set Combinations

Passport holders can pair with luggage tags, pens, notebooks, travel pouches, welcome cards or custom packaging when the buyer wants a complete travel kit.

Logo, MOQ, Sampling, Packaging and Shipping

Logo Customization

Logo methods may include embossing, debossing, foil stamping or printing. The preferred option depends on brand style, logo detail, PU texture and whether a subtle or high-contrast result is needed.

MOQ Factors

Many standard stock-color projects may start from approximately 100 pieces, but final MOQ depends on passport cover style, color, logo method, packaging and whether the order includes paired travel accessories.

Sample Approval

Sample review should confirm cover size, pocket layout, logo placement, foil color if used, stitching and packaging fit before bulk production starts.

Packing and Shipping

Passport holders can be individually bagged, boxed or combined with travel kits. International shipping planning needs destination, postal code, quantity, target date and shipment address details.

What material is common for custom passport holders?

Custom Branding Options

A good exhibition giveaway should be useful, easy to carry and strong in logo visibility.

Tote Bag Branding

Tote bags can support screen printing, heat transfer, woven label, embroidery patch or full-color printing depending on material and design.

Notebook and Pen Logo

Notebooks and pens can be customized by printing, foil stamping, debossing, laser engraving or full-color logo methods.

Lanyard and Badge Holder

Lanyards and badge holders are practical for events and help separate attendees, staff, VIPs, speakers and volunteers.

Insert Card and Packaging

Add event agenda cards, QR code cards, sponsor cards, paper bags, box sleeves, stickers or branded packaging labels.

How to Use This Gallery for Your Project

Match the Use Case

Start by choosing the business moment: employee onboarding, client appreciation, conference handout, holiday gift or executive relationship building. Each use case needs a different product value, packaging level and delivery plan.

Confirm Logo Methods

Logo placement can be planned across the product, box, sleeve, paper bag, insert card and shipping carton. Printing, engraving, embroidery and labels should be selected by material, quantity and desired brand effect.

Plan Packaging Early

Gift box size, insert structure and carton packing affect both presentation and freight cost. Share product dimensions, target budget and destination country early so packaging can be quoted realistically.

Request Similar Gift Set Ideas

When you reference a gallery image, include the estimated quantity, preferred product quality, logo color, packaging style and delivery country. This lets us suggest similar items and explain which details may change because of MOQ, available stock colors or shipping volume.

For premium boxes, ask for sample confirmation before bulk production. For event and employee kits, confirm carton packing and handout method so the final sets are easy to distribute with fewer surprises.

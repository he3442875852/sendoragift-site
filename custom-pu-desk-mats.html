Procurement Notes for PU Desk Mats

A desk mat should be selected for size, color, edge finish, surface texture and logo method before pricing. Standard ready-made desk mats are generally easier to source than special structures or custom functions.

This page does not assume wireless charging or other special structures. If a buyer needs a special feature, it should be provided as a separate specification and quoted only after confirmation.

Quote Inputs Buyers Should Prepare

  • Preferred size such as 60 x 30 cm, 80 x 40 cm or 90 x 45 cm
  • Stock color or brand color direction
  • Logo method and placement
  • Single item or part of an employee kit
  • Packaging and delivery destination

Use Cases, Sizes and Product Styles

Office and Remote Work Gifts

PU desk mats are useful for employee welcome kits, remote team gifts, office refresh programs and client desktop gifts. They provide daily visibility without requiring a complex product structure.

Common Size Planning

Common planning sizes include 60 x 30 cm, 80 x 40 cm and 90 x 45 cm, subject to product availability. Buyers should confirm desk space, shipping carton limits and whether the mat ships alone or inside a gift box.

Standard Ready-Made Mats

Sendora Gift mainly uses standard ready-made desk mats customized with logo or packaging. This helps keep product sourcing clear and avoids unverified claims about special functions.

Colors and Finish

Black, brown, grey, navy and neutral office colors are often considered for business programs. Final color availability should be confirmed before sample or bulk quotation.

Logo, MOQ, Sampling, Packaging and Shipping

Logo Customization

Logo methods may include printing, debossing, embossing or foil stamping depending on the PU surface and artwork. A subtle corner logo is often preferred for corporate office use.

MOQ Factors

Many standard stock-color projects may start from approximately 100 pieces, but final MOQ depends on desk mat size, color, logo method, packaging and whether the order includes other gift-set items.

Sample Review

A sample should confirm mat size, surface feel, logo location, logo sharpness, edge finish and packaging fit. Large desk mats should also be reviewed for carton size and shipping volume.

Packing and Shipping

Desk mats can be individually bagged, rolled, flat packed or combined with notebooks and pens if packaging allows. International shipping needs destination, postal code, carton count and target arrival date.

Buyer FAQ

What sizes are common for PU desk mats?

Common planning sizes include 60 x 30 cm, 80 x 40 cm and 90 x 45 cm, subject to current product availability and packing requirements.

Are these custom-made desk mats?

The usual approach is to use standard ready-made desk mats and customize them with logo or packaging. Special structures need separate confirmation.

Can desk mats be included in employee welcome kits?

Yes. Desk mats can be combined with notebooks, pens, drinkware, cards or packaging if the size and shipping plan are practical.

Can Sendora Gift confirm MOQ before sampling?

Yes. The team can review product direction, color, logo method and packaging first, then confirm the practical MOQ for that exact combination. Many standard stock-color projects may start from approximately 100 pieces, but final MOQ depends on the selected product, color, logo method and packaging.

Can buyers approve a sample before bulk production?

Yes. Buyers can request a sample, sample photo or pre-production confirmation depending on product type, timeline and logo method. Bulk production should start after the approved details are clear.

What information is needed for international shipping?

Please provide destination country, postal code, quantity, expected carton limits if any, target delivery date and whether the shipment should go to one address or multiple addresses.

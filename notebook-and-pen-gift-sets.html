Notebook and Pen Set Buying Guide

Notebook and pen gift sets are reliable choices for conferences, employee onboarding, training programs and client meetings. They are compact, budget-friendly and easy to combine with other branded items.

Notebook Options

Choose A5 or B5 notebooks, hard covers, soft covers, recycled paper, elastic bands, pen loops, ribbon markers or custom inner pages depending on the program.

Pen Options

Metal pens, gel pens and stylus pens can be printed or engraved. Matching pen color with the notebook or packaging creates a more polished set.

Packaging Options

Use paper sleeves, belly bands, rigid boxes, kraft boxes or branded cards to make simple stationery feel more complete.

Best Use Cases

  • Conference registration packs and training materials.
  • Employee welcome kits with notebooks, pens, drinkware and bags.
  • Client meeting gifts with subtle logo branding and premium packaging.

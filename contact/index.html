What to Include in Your Corporate Gift Brief

You do not need a final product list before contacting us. To recommend practical corporate gift options, we usually need your estimated quantity, recipient type, target budget, delivery country, deadline and logo or packaging requirements.

If you already have a reference photo, brand color, logo file or preferred product category, include it in the brief. These details help us suggest a realistic product mix, suitable branding method, sample direction and bulk production schedule.

For employee gifts, tell us whether the project is for onboarding, appreciation, remote teams or an internal event.
For client gifts, share the relationship level, presentation expectation and whether a greeting card or premium box is needed.
For event giveaways, include the event date, visitor type, booth distribution method and latest arrival date.

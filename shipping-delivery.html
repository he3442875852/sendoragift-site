Shipping Options

The right shipping method depends on delivery country, carton volume, deadline and budget. We can help compare options during quotation.

Express Delivery

Suitable for samples, urgent small batches and time-sensitive event gifts.

Air Freight

Useful when bulk orders need faster delivery than sea freight but have larger carton volume than express.

Sea Freight

Often practical for larger bulk orders when the project has a longer planning timeline.

Door-to-Door Options

Can you ship worldwide?

Yes. Shipping options can be discussed based on destination, timing, product type and order volume.

Can gifts ship directly to an event?

In many cases yes, but event venue receiving rules and deadline buffers should be confirmed early.

Can you estimate shipping cost?

Shipping Options for Corporate Gift Projects

Shipping should be planned together with production, especially for gift sets with bulky packaging or multiple delivery destinations. The right method depends on deadline, carton volume, destination and budget.

Express Delivery

Best for urgent samples, smaller quantities and time-sensitive event materials when speed is more important than freight cost.

Air Freight

Suitable for medium-size orders with fixed event dates, product launches or employee onboarding schedules.

Sea Freight

Useful for larger bulk orders where buyers can plan ahead and want a more economical freight option.

Delivery Details to Confirm

  • Destination country, city, postal code, delivery address and contact person.
  • Event date, latest arrival date and whether split shipment is required.
  • Carton labels, packing list, product HS code needs and any special documentation.

Decisions to make before sampling

Document wallet fit

Measure the largest sheet, folded booklet and total stack thickness. A4 and US Letter documents are not the same size. Share whether manuals stay flat or folded, and confirm usable internal dimensions rather than relying only on the outer size.

Keyring and pen details

Select the ring diameter, strap or metal body, finish and logo area. Check that the key fob can be attached without forcing the ring. For the pen, approve the barrel logo direction, writing color, refill and case fit.

Logo and presentation

Use your dealership artwork on the wallet, keyring, pen or box. Printing, debossing and engraving depend on the selected surface. Review small lettering at actual size; a digital mockup alone cannot confirm the final texture or metal finish.

Match packaging to storage and delivery

For a handover desk, a shallow presentation box can make each piece easy to lift out. For regular branch replenishment, compare an individual wallet pack with a full gift box: box dimensions, protective packing and carton quantity affect storage space and freight. Do not set a box height until the actual wallet, pen and keyring have been measured together.

Buyer questions

What should go in a car dealership handover gift set?

Start with a PU document wallet, keyring and thank-you card. Add a metal pen and gift box when the signing and presentation experience is part of the brief. The final mix should fit the documents, key fob, storage space and budget.

Can the document wallet hold A4 or US Letter paperwork?

The wallet must be checked against your actual documents. Share the largest sheet dimensions, booklet thickness and whether papers may be folded. A sample should confirm usable internal space, closure and access before bulk production.

Can the dealer logo appear on every item?

Logo placement can be reviewed on the document wallet, keyring, pen, card and box. The suitable method and readable artwork size vary by material. Provide your authorized logo file and approve each placement before production.

What is the minimum order for custom dealership gifts?

Many standard gift-set projects are planned from around 100 sets. The confirmed minimum depends on the wallet, keyring, stock color, branding method and packaging. Splitting the order across different branch logos may change setup costs or minimum quantities.

Can we review a sample before placing a bulk order?

Sample options, cost and timing are confirmed after the components and artwork are selected. Review the wallet with your documents, the keyring with the intended fob, the pen logo direction and the full packed layout.

Can we reorder the same kit for several dealerships?

A repeat-order brief should retain the approved component specifications, artwork version, packing layout and branch labels. Availability, color matching and production timing must be reconfirmed for each order; keeping an approved physical sample helps the comparison.

Prepare your gift quote

Send the number of handovers or sets, document dimensions, keyring style, pen preference, logo file, target budget, delivery country and postal code. Include the first required arrival date and any split between branches. Tell us whether your budget includes packaging and freight so the options can be compared on the same basis.

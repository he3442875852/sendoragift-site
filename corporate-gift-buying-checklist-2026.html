What to Confirm Before Requesting a Quote

A clear buying brief saves time because MOQ, pricing and timing depend on the exact product, color, logo method, packaging and destination. The checklist below helps teams prepare the details that suppliers need for a realistic quotation.

The year matters because project schedules, event calendars and budget cycles change. The procurement basics remain practical: define recipients, product direction, quantity, artwork, packing and shipping.

Quote Inputs Buyers Should Prepare

  • Recipient group and project purpose
  • Estimated quantity and target budget
  • Product direction and logo artwork
  • Sample requirement and approval owner
  • Packaging, destination and target delivery date

Checklist for Procurement Teams

Recipient and Use Case

Define whether the gift is for employees, clients, event attendees, distributors, schools or travel programs. Recipient context affects product size, perceived value, packaging and shipping method.

Product and Logo Direction

Choose product categories before asking for final pricing. Logo method should be reviewed by material, shape, logo detail and color contrast.

MOQ and Sample Control

Ask for MOQ based on the exact selected product and logo method. Confirm whether a physical sample, sample photo or digital mockup is needed before bulk production.

Shipping Information

Provide destination country, postal code, target date, carton rules and whether goods ship to one warehouse, office, event venue or multiple addresses.

Buyer FAQ

What should be included in a corporate gift brief?

Include recipient type, quantity, target budget, product direction, logo file, packaging requirement, delivery country, target date and sample expectation.

Why is MOQ not fixed?

MOQ depends on selected product, color, logo method, packaging and schedule. A standard stock-color product can differ from a special color or custom package.

Should buyers approve samples?

Sample approval is recommended when logo, packaging or color accuracy matters to the project.

What shipping details are needed?

Destination country, postal code, quantity, target delivery date, carton rules and address type are needed for shipping planning.

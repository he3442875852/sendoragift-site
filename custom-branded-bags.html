Choosing Custom Branded Bags

Bags are useful because they work both as packaging and as a long-term branded item. The best choice depends on weight, budget, event format and how recipients will use the bag after receiving it.

Tote Bags

Cotton, canvas and non-woven tote bags are popular for trade shows, schools, welcome kits and eco friendly campaigns.

Backpacks

Backpacks are better for employee onboarding, tech teams, student programs and premium event kits where laptop storage matters.

Drawstring Bags

Drawstring bags suit sports events, youth programs, races and campaigns that need lightweight, cost-effective packaging.

Branding and Packing Tips

  • Confirm print size, fabric color, handle length and bag weight before sampling.
  • Use inner packaging or hang tags when bags are part of a premium set.
  • Check carton quantity and folding method so bags arrive clean and easy to distribute.

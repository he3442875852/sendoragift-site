Procurement Notes for Custom Lanyards

For most event and employee programs, a practical starting point is a polyester lanyard in a standard stock color with one or two logo positions. Wider lanyards can give more room for readable artwork, while narrower versions may reduce weight and cost for high-volume distribution.

Buyers should confirm where the lanyard will be used: conference registration, school ID programs, employee access, visitor badges or promotional kits. The use case affects width, clip type, safety buckle and packaging.

Quote Inputs Buyers Should Prepare

  • Quantity and event date
  • Preferred width such as 15 mm or 20 mm
  • Stock color or brand color direction
  • Logo artwork and print color count
  • Clip type, safety buckle need and badge holder pairing

Product Choices and Common Uses

Polyester Lanyards

Polyester lanyards are common for B2B programs because they are lightweight, easy to print and suitable for conference check-in, school ID cards, employee badges and visitor passes. Buyers can select standard stock colors first, then review logo contrast and repeat pattern direction.

15 mm and 20 mm Widths

15 mm and 20 mm are common planning widths. A 15 mm lanyard can be compact and economical for simple logos, while 20 mm can give more printable area for event names, sponsor logos or longer text.

Clips and Safety Buckles

Clip options may include metal hooks, swivel hooks, badge clips or phone loops depending on the accessory. A safety buckle can be considered for schools, factories, events or employee programs where quick release is preferred.

Conference and School Use

For conferences, lanyards often pair with badge holders, event cards or welcome kits. For schools and universities, durability, safety buckle selection and card holder size should be confirmed before quoting.

Logo, MOQ, Samples, Packing and Shipping

Logo Customization Methods

Screen printing is suitable for simple artwork and readable repeat logos. Heat transfer printing can be considered when artwork needs more color detail. Final method depends on logo size, color count, strap color and quantity.

Color and MOQ Factors

Standard stock colors are usually easier to source than custom-dyed straps. Many standard stock-color projects may start from approximately 100 pieces, but final MOQ depends on the lanyard width, color, logo method, clip, safety buckle and packaging.

Sample Review

A sample or sample photo should confirm strap color, print sharpness, logo repeat direction, clip type, safety buckle placement and whether the lanyard matches the badge holder or card size.

Bulk Packing and Shipping

Packing can be bulk packed, individually bagged or grouped with badge holders and event kits. International shipping needs destination country, postal code, delivery deadline, carton limits and whether goods ship to an event venue or office.

Buyer FAQ

What width is common for custom lanyards?

15 mm and 20 mm are common widths. The better choice depends on logo readability, event style, budget and accessory weight.

Can lanyards include a safety buckle?

Yes, a safety buckle can be discussed when quick release is preferred for schools, employees, factories or large events.

Which logo method is used on polyester lanyards?

Screen printing and heat transfer printing are common. The final method depends on artwork detail, color count, strap color and quantity.

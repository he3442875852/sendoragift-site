FAQ

Pickleball Team Gift Questions

Can I change the two-paddle and four-ball combination?

The displayed combination is a starting point. Specify the number of paddles and balls per kit and whether you need a carry bag or box. The final contents, minimum quantity and packaging fit are checked for that configuration.

What paddle details should I confirm before ordering?

Specify the intended use, face and core materials, thickness, dimensions, weight range and grip requirements. Review the actual product specification and a sample; the appearance of a paddle in a photo does not establish those technical details.

Are the paddles approved for sanctioned tournament play?

No tournament approval is claimed on this page. If a specific approval is required, identify the governing body and exact model requirement before selection, and verify the applicable documentation for that model before placing an order.

Should I request indoor or outdoor pickleballs?

State the intended court environment and any required ball specification. Ask for the selected ball details and review a sample for your use. The displayed ball count or color does not establish suitability for every court or event.

Can I put my own artwork on the paddles and carry bag?

Share your own artwork and the desired placement on each surface. Decoration area, method and color appearance are reviewed for the selected paddle and bag. Approve their layouts separately, including how the artwork sits near the grip and edges.

What should I check on a complete pickleball gift sample?

Check paddle dimensions and grip, the selected balls, bag capacity, logo placement and protection during packing. Include a use review against your stated requirements. Sample cost and timing are confirmed separately from bulk production.

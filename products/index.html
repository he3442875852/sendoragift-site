Product Selection FAQ

Not sure which category fits your project? These answers help buyers prepare a faster inquiry.

How do I choose the right corporate gift category?

Start with the recipient and use case. Employee programs often need welcome kits, events need practical giveaways, and VIP clients may need premium gift boxes with stronger packaging.

Can you recommend products by budget?

Yes. Share quantity, target budget, delivery country and event date, and we can recommend suitable product combinations and packaging options.

Can each category include logo customization?

Most categories can support logo customization. Common methods include printing, laser engraving, embroidery, UV printing and branded packaging.

Do I need a final product list before sending an inquiry?

No. You can send your recipient type, quantity, budget and deadline first. We can help narrow the product mix before quotation.

Need a Custom Product Mix?

Tell us your recipient, quantity, budget and logo need. We can recommend a suitable product combination.

What is included in the gym welcome kit?

The combination shown in the supplied video includes a water bottle, drawstring bag, wristbands and a welcome card. Final models and the quantity of each component are confirmed in the quote.

Is a sports towel included?

A towel is an optional addition. It is not included in the displayed bottle, bag, wristbands and welcome-card combination.

Can the products use our gym logo?

Coordinated logo customization can be planned for the selected bottle, bag and wristbands, with printed artwork on the card. Feasible methods depend on the actual materials and design.

Can the welcome card include our QR code?

What is the minimum order quantity?

The minimum order is confirmed for the selected bottle, bag, wristbands, printed card and packing. Share your planned membership intake quantity and any split between club locations for review.

Can we plan an opening date or multiple club deliveries?

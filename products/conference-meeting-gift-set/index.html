FAQ

Conference Gift Set Questions

Should the folder fit A4 or US Letter documents?

Use the actual documents you plan to distribute as the size reference. Confirm usable pocket dimensions, closure and the thickness of the full document stack. A format name alone does not confirm that your printed agenda and inserts will fit.

What badge details are needed for a conference kit?

Provide badge width, height, portrait or landscape orientation and the intended attachment. Confirm whether the badge holder must be rigid or flexible, and check the lanyard clip and any safety-release requirement before selecting the components.

Can sponsor and attendee versions use different artwork?

List each version, its quantity and the artwork for every affected item. Confirm the allocation before sampling. Separate print runs, colors or packaging versions can affect minimum quantities and preparation time.

Can the kits arrive ready for attendee check-in?

Request pre-packed kits in the brief and specify the exact contents and packing order. Confirm carton labels and quantities per carton so staff can identify each version. Assembly arrangements and costs are confirmed with the selected items.

Which date should I use when requesting an event quote?

Give both the event date and the latest acceptable receiving date at the venue or office. Include any delivery window, appointment or receiving restriction. The plan needs time for artwork approval, sampling if required, production, shipping and receiving.

What should I approve on a conference-kit sample?

Check document fit, writing items, badge orientation, lanyard attachment, logo readability and the packed arrangement. Approve each sponsor version separately where the artwork or contents differ.

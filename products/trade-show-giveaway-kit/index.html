FAQ

Trade Show Giveaway Questions

Should I use one giveaway for every booth visitor?

You can brief a simple handout for general visitors and a separate kit for booked meetings or priority contacts. Keep the quantities and contents of each version clear so the quotation and carton labels support your distribution plan.

How can I reduce shipping bulk for a trade-show kit?

Start with compact products and compare their packed dimensions. Ask whether flat bags, smaller notebooks or fewer items suit the brief. Compare the total shipment volume and protection needs before deciding that a lighter-looking kit will cost less to send.

Can the tote carry the entire giveaway kit?

Confirm the tote dimensions, handle length and the weight of the chosen items. Check a packed sample, including any event literature you will add. A bag image alone does not confirm capacity or suitability for the final contents.

Should giveaways be packed as kits or supplied separately?

Pre-packed kits can simplify check-in or scheduled meetings. Separate items give booth staff flexibility during handouts. Ask for both packing scopes if needed and include assembly, cartons and venue handling when comparing the quotes.

What information is needed for delivery to an exhibition?

Provide the country, venue receiving address, latest receiving date and any booking or labeling instructions. Confirm whether a warehouse, forwarder or event organizer will receive the cartons. The show date alone is not enough to plan delivery.

How should I allocate the giveaway budget?

Separate the allowance for products, logo decoration, packaging and delivery. State whether your target is per item, per finished kit or for the whole project. This allows the product mix to be adjusted without hiding a cost outside the comparison.

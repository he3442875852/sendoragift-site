FAQ

Golf Tournament Gift Questions

Can I request a small ball-and-tee pack instead of a full gift box?

Yes, describe the number of balls and tees you want per pack and whether a marker or card is included. Sleeve, tube, pouch and box directions can be reviewed against those contents. Minimums and costs depend on the selected components and branding.

Can the ball, towel and packaging carry different sponsor logos?

Provide the artwork and quantity for each placement. The ball, textile and packaging use different surfaces and decoration processes, so each layout needs its own approval. Ask for a physical sample where appearance or color matching is important.

Does a golf-ball sample establish a particular performance grade?

No. A sample photo shows appearance and presentation, not a verified performance specification. State the ball construction or performance requirements in your brief and request the relevant product details before choosing it for the event.

How do I choose between a sleeve, tube and presentation box?

A sleeve or tube can suit a compact ball pack, while a box can hold a larger accessory combination. Confirm the number and dimensions of the items, then compare fit, protection, decoration and packed volume before selecting the format.

Can I add a cap, towel or ball marker to the set?

Include the requested accessory, quantity, material or size preference and logo position in the brief. An extra item changes the box or bag fit and can introduce a separate minimum quantity or decoration step.

What dates do you need for a golf tournament order?

Share the event date and the latest receiving date at the club, office or forwarder. Allow for artwork and sample approval before production. The schedule and shipping options are confirmed for your final items, quantity and destination.

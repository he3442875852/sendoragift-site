FAQ

Client Appreciation Gift Questions

Can I brand only the box and keep the products plain?

Request unbranded products with a logo on the box, sleeve or card. Product availability and packaging minimums still need to be checked. This is a useful direction when you want a personal thank-you message with restrained branding.

How do I make a small client gift box feel coordinated?

Start with a limited product mix and consistent colors. Confirm the mug, notebook and pen finishes together, then check the insert spacing and card position. The full presentation depends on fit and finish as well as the number of items.

Can I replace the ceramic mug for easier shipping?

Ask for an alternative drinkware item or a smaller office accessory. Compare the final packed weight, dimensions and protective materials. A replacement changes the box fit and may change the logo method and minimum quantity.

Can each client receive a different thank-you card?

Provide the number of versions and the final approved wording for each. Individual names and messages require a checked personalization list. Confirm the process, cost and timing before promising personalized delivery to your recipients.

Does a quoted gift-box price include delivery and sample costs?

Check the scope of the quotation. Ask for product and decoration costs, packaging, samples and delivery to be identified, and confirm how any destination charges are handled. Share the delivery country and quantity for a useful comparison.

Do the sample photos confirm the exact box I will receive?

They are references for product mix, colors and presentation. Your final box dimensions, materials, insert, artwork and products are specified in the quotation and approval record. Use a sample to check the details that matter to your project.

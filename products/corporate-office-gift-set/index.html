FAQ

Office Gift Set Questions

Can I replace the tumbler with a ceramic mug?

Yes, request a mug as an alternative in your brief. Its handle, height and weight change the space and protection needed in the box, so the insert and shipping quote must be reviewed with the new item.

What notebook and pen details should I specify?

For the notebook, confirm size, cover material, page count and ruling. For the pen, confirm barrel finish, ink color and refill type. Include the intended logo position on each item so the quote covers the same specification you want to approve.

Will the same logo look identical on the notebook, pen and tumbler?

The appearance can differ because the surfaces and decoration methods differ. Review a separate artwork layout for each item and use a physical sample when color, engraving contrast or a recessed finish matters.

Should I approve one item or the complete boxed set?

A complete boxed sample lets you check item fit, spacing, logo orientation and protection together. An individual item sample is useful for its own finish, but does not confirm how the full set will fit in the selected box.

How many office gift sets should I plan for?

Around 100 sets is a typical planning starting point. The confirmed minimum depends on the notebook, drinkware, pen, stock colors, decoration and box. Share your actual quantity so each component can be checked before you commit.

What should I keep for a repeat office gift order?

Keep the approved item list, dimensions, color references, artwork files and photos of the finished set. Include the box and insert specification. Availability and any proposed substitutions should be reconfirmed for the next batch.
